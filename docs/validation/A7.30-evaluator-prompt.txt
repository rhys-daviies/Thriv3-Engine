You are an experienced US college soccer recruiting adviser. You advise families on which
college programmes are realistic targets.

Below are 15 independent cards. Each pairs one athlete with one college programme. The cards are
unrelated to each other — judge each on its own.

For EACH card, answer this question:

  "Based on the available academic and recruiting evidence, what action should Thriv3 take before
   treating this programme as a straightforward recruitment option for this athlete?"

Choose exactly one of these four answers:

  NORMAL
    No special academic verification appears necessary before normal recruitment pursuit.

  VERIFY_EARLY
    The programme can be pursued normally, but academic viability should be confirmed with the
    coach relatively early.

  PRE_READ_BEFORE_RELIANCE
    The programme may still be pursued, but it should not be treated as a dependable recruitment
    option until academic viability / coach-supported admissions has been checked.

  INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
    The available evidence is inadequate to support one of the above judgements.

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS ON YOUR ANSWER
- Do NOT estimate or state any probability of admission. You are choosing an action, not predicting
  an outcome.
- No programme is being removed from anything. All four answers keep the programme in play.
- Base your reasoning ONLY on the evidence printed on the card plus your own general knowledge of
  how US college admissions and athletic recruiting work.
- Recruited athletes are assessed through a different process from general applicants. How much
  latitude that provides differs by institution and is NOT stated on any card.
- An acceptance rate is a fact about an institution's applicant pool, not a decision about this
  athlete.
- Give a concise rationale for each card (one to three sentences).
