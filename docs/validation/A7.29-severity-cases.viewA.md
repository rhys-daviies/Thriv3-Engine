# A7.29 BLIND REVIEW — what action should be taken first?

**This is a blind review.** Each card is one athlete paired with one real programme. Nothing here
tells you what any assessment concluded, how the cards were chosen, or what distinguishes them.
The codes are in an arbitrary order.

One question per card:

> Based on the academic and recruiting evidence available, what action should Thriv3 take before
> treating this programme as a straightforward recruitment option for this athlete?

Choose one:

- **NORMAL** — academics do not require any special action before normal pursuit.
- **VERIFY_EARLY** — pursue normally, but confirm academic viability with the coach relatively
  early in the process.
- **PRE_READ_BEFORE_RELIANCE** — the programme can still be pursued, but it should not be treated
  as a dependable option until academic viability or coach support has been checked.
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** — the facts here do not support choosing between the above.

**No programme is being removed and none of these is a prediction.** You are not being asked how
likely admission is and should not estimate it. The question is only which of these four actions
the evidence warrants.

Worth holding in mind: an acceptance rate describes an applicant pool, not a decision about this
athlete; a test-score difference is evidence, not an outcome; and recruited athletes are assessed
through a different process from general applicants, by an amount that differs by institution and
is not stated anywhere in this document.
