# A8.2 — majorFit evidence-semantics repair: result

A8.1 is **not** superseded. It identified this defect against the pre-repair
engine and remains the record of that finding; `A8.0-baseline.json` and
`A8.0B-extension.json` are untouched historical instruments. This phase adds
`A8.2-extension.json` beside them.

## The defect

`majorFit` scored a **MEASURED 0 at coverage 1** when an athlete's declared
major was absent from `colleges.notable_majors`, under a comment reading
*"Nothing is inferred."*

It was inferred. `notable_majors` comes from College Scorecard **PCIP
completion shares** and names an institution's largest fields of study — a mean
of **7.55 of 14** families. **321 of 349** NCAA D1 women's programmes omit
Mathematics, among them Penn State, Ohio State, Wisconsin, Michigan State,
Arizona State, Minnesota, Rutgers and Texas A&M. All grant mathematics degrees.

The belief came from `docs/L2_ACADEMIC_CONTEXT.md` — "the programme half is
essentially complete" — which means 98% of programmes *carry a value*, not that
the lists are catalogues. The same page prints the refutation two paragraphs
later: Mathematics 6.9%, English 15.4%.

Evidence classification: **`PARTIAL_POSITIVE_ONLY`**. No authoritative negative
source exists anywhere in the repository — no catalogue table, no CIP table, no
"not offered" field. `docs/matchmaking-v2-spec.md` already graded the field
**D**, the weakest grade, while the engine graded a derived non-match MEASURED.

## The repair

One branch. Absence from a partial list now **refuses** instead of scoring
zero, with its own reason so an explanation can distinguish "no list recorded"
from "a list that does not settle it".

| situation | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| no intended major | NOT_APPLICABLE | unchanged |
| major named in the list | 1, MEASURED, cov 1 | **unchanged** |
| **absent from a partial list** | **0, MEASURED, cov 1** | **UNSCOREABLE `MAJOR_NOT_IN_PARTIAL_EVIDENCE`** |
| no list at all | UNSCOREABLE `NO_PROGRAMME_MAJOR_EVIDENCE` | unchanged |
| synonym match | 1 | unchanged |
| unplaceable/undecided major | NOT_APPLICABLE | unchanged |
| malformed evidence | UNSCOREABLE | unchanged |
| authoritative negative | — | **not implemented — no such evidence exists** |

No weight changed. majorFit is still 1.2; Opportunity, Pursuit, Recruitability,
Financial, Playing Pathway and Candidate G are untouched.

**Why UNSCOREABLE rather than NOT_APPLICABLE.** Both satisfy "no measured
zero", and both yield an *identical Opportunity value*. They differ only in
coverage. `notApplicable` means the question does not apply; `unscoreable`
means it applies and the evidence is missing — which is the truth here, because
the athlete did ask. The decisive argument is internal consistency: an **empty**
list already refused, and a **short** list that omits the major is the same
unknown. Same state, different reason.

## Opportunity composition — measured before implementing

`majorFit` is not `required`, so `combine` renormalises. An unscoreable
component stays in the denominator, so coverage falls by exactly its share.

| candidate | new coverage | cells losing rankability |
|---|---|---|
| **UNSCOREABLE (chosen)** | median 0.455 | **14** |
| NOT_APPLICABLE | median 1.000 | 0 |

Predicted 19 at-risk; 5 were already LIMITED_DATA, so **14** newly drop —
exactly matching the observed 1,099 → 1,085. Every one of the 14 already lacked
win-rate history, leaving only Playing Pathway assembled: 0.65 of 2.2, coverage
0.295, below the frozen floor of 0.35. They sat at ranks 462–714 and are now
LIMITED_DATA — a visible, truthful state. That is the coverage floor working on
genuinely thin evidence, not collateral damage.

### The mechanics, not the recovery

A non-match programme's Opportunity is now **numerically identical** to its
value had no major been stated — Chicago 0.8142 = 0.8142, Rose-Hulman 0.5343 =
0.5343, Washington and Lee 0.6802 = 0.6802 — with coverage 0.455 honestly
recording the unanswered question. That identity *is* the repair: the unknown
is neutral.

Ranks do **not** return to the no-major reference, and should not. The 569
programmes with a named positive match keep a 0.545-share value of 1, so a
non-match is ranked below known-good fits without being pushed below anything
by an invented negative.

**A correction to A8.1.** That report named Rose-Hulman as a headline victim at

the rest is Financial — F = 0.336, an expensive private for a $20–25k family.
Washington and Lee, at 106 places, was the genuine case. The defect was real and
the measurement of its size was right; that one example was poorly chosen.

Clarkson and Michigan Tech moved *down*. Their own evidence did not change —
the field around them did, as programmes with positive matches rose. The
classification changed because the evidence is truthful now, not because ranks
recovered.

## Tests

New truth table: 11 rows covering no major, positive match, partial-list
absence, no evidence, synonym, unplaceable major, malformed evidence, the
standing "never 0" assertion, the explanation wording, and the absence of an
authoritative-negative branch.

**Mutation-tested.** Restoring the defect — partial-list absence → measured
zero — makes T3, T5, T9 and T11 fail; the module still loads, so the failure is
behavioural rather than a syntax error.

Four pre-existing tests encoded the defect and were updated, not deleted:

- `majorFit.test.js` "produces only 0, 1 or no answer" → "produces 1 or no
  answer, and never 0". Its comment calling the field "a course catalogue" is
  corrected — that premise was the defect.
- `opportunity.test.js` "a matched major raises it and a missed one lowers it"
  → "…and an unanswerable one leaves it alone", asserting `unknown === none`.
- `opportunityComponents.test.js` "scores a measured zero where the programme
  does not teach it" → "refuses where the list exists but does not settle it".
- `opportunityRun.test.js` majorFit coverage `> 0.9` → strictly between 0 and
  1: the component now scores only where the major is named, and that share is
  the honest number.

Frozen architecture parity: A7.44 (40), A7.45 (24), A7.46 (16), A7.48 (24),
Candidate G (22), V1 bridge (56), eligibility (63), import graph (6), A8
structural (32), A8.0B invariants (19) — all pass. `scan:committed-pii` clean,
`snapshot:pi` 0 differences, `snapshot:matching` 359 fields (unchanged corpus
drift). Full suite **3 failures, 0 new**, 10,149 passing.

## What remains unknown

Thriv3 still cannot say whether a programme offers a given major. The repair
removes a false claim; it does not add knowledge. A genuine catalogue — IPEDS
completions at CIP-code level, or institutional programme listings — would let
`majorFit` answer for the 539 cells it now refuses, and would make an
authoritative-negative branch legitimate. Test T11 guards that boundary and is
written to fail when such a source arrives, sending the next reader to the
preregistration.

Until then: positive major evidence carries authority, an unanswerable question
carries none, and nothing carries a negative.
