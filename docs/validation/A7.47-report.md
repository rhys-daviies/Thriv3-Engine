## Explanation defects — the reason this audit does not close the phase

Numeric output, grade and refusal state tell the same story everywhere. The
sentences do not.

**D1 — `NO_MINUTES_HISTORY` describes a departing cohort that does not exist.
2,280 cells (21.7%), 570 programmes.** A programme with no roster at all is
explained as *"nobody leaving this position could be placed as a starter or a
squad player…"*. One reason code carries two meanings: `squadRotation` emits it
for "no roster on file", `positionalOpportunity` for the departing-cohort case,
and the single refusal phrase was written for the second.

**D2 — `NO_ROSTER_ON_FILE` claims no roster when one is held. 6 cells.** A
programme with 14 roster rows and nobody at goalkeeper is explained as *"Thriv3
holds no current roster for this programme."* A7.44 made the states tellable
apart by reason and left the phrase false.

**D3 — `PATHWAY_NOT_RANKED` promises rotation evidence that does not exist.
Same 2,280 cells.** The sentence says what is known about rotation "is kept and
reported" even where rotation itself refused. D1 and D3 compound on identical
cells.

V2 serves no live surface, so no athlete has read these. **17 committed review
packs contain the phrases**, and those packs were generated 2026-09-23 from
`227b54f` — before A7.44, A7.45 and A7.46 — so they are stale as well as
misleading.
