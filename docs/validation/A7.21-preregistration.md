# A7.21 — preregistration

**Written and committed before any candidate was measured.** Baselines quoted are A7.20's, already
committed. Nothing in this phase changes production.

---

## 1. The defect, restated from A7.20

Playing and academic preferences at 5/5 change **9–18 of 100** programmes against competitive
level's **60–80**. Direction is correct everywhere (playing 1→5 brings in pathway 0.66 against
exits 0.41; academic brings in rating 7.1 against 2.6), monotonicity holds, nothing is excluded.
This is an **authority** defect.

A7.20 established where the authority is lost, by eliminating the alternatives:

| experiment | playing churn | conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| current | 8–12 | — |
| weight range widened 8× inside Opportunity | **7–12** | re-weighting inside O does nothing |
| renormalisation removed entirely | **9–12** | renormalisation is not the attenuator |
| bounded preference multiplier on O | 9–11 | not the attenuator |
| **multipliers applied post-gate** | **24–32** | placement is the attenuator |

`O` is bounded [0,1] and contributes at most **0.30** to base, so its whole p10–p90 swing is ~0.12
of Pursuit against the level anchor's 0.28–0.47. **Essentially all of the authority difference is
architectural placement.** Competitive level got its authority by leaving Opportunity.

One further finding, load-bearing below: `majorFit` receives **no** multiplier from the 1–5 scale.
`PRIORITY_MAP` routes the legacy `criterion_ranking` key `academic → majorFit`, while explicit
`academicStrengthPriority` routes to `academicStrengthFit`. A declared major is weighted identically
whether the athlete rates academics 1 or 5.

## 2. Locked operator decisions

1. Competitive level remains the strongest anchor. Candidate G's level anchor is not weakened to
   equalise preferences.
2. Playing and academic at 5/5 must materially reshape the list. **25–35 top-100 churn is a healthy
   reference region, not a target.** Identity and direction of movers matter more than raw churn.
3. **τ(3) = 0.** Priority 3 is neutral and must be mathematically inert. The function is genuinely
   centred: 1–2 favour the opposite trade-off, 3 nothing, 4–5 favour the objective. **Not
   subtract-only.**
4. A declared major is direct fit information, not academic prestige and not a filter.
5. `majorFit` stays honest binary. No semantic similarity invented in this phase.

## 3. Centred preference semantics

Every preference candidate below is an **interaction**, never a constant on the athlete side, since
a constant cannot reorder anything:

```
direction(p) = (p − 3) / 2        →  1: −1.0   2: −0.5   3: 0   4: +0.5   5: +1.0
adjustment   = 1 + κ · direction(p) · (v − v̄)
```

`v` is the programme-side signal (`playingPathway`, `academicStrengthFit`, `majorFit`) and `v̄` its
**pool median for this athlete**, computed over the ranked universe, so the adjustment is centred on
the pool rather than on an invented constant. At priority 3 the factor is exactly 1 for every
programme. At 5 a programme above the pool median gains and one below loses; at 1 that reverses, so
an athlete who says playing time matters least stops paying a premium for it — which is the
operator's stated requirement, not a penalty.

## 4. Candidates

`v̄` = pool median of the signal. All are bounded and strictly positive.

| id | formula | notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CONTROL** | production as shipped | — |
| **P1** | `P × (1 + κ·d(playing)·(pathway − v̄))` | playing only; κ ∈ {0.8, 1.6, 2.4} |
| **P2** | `P × (1 + κ·d(academic)·(academicFit − v̄))` | academic only; same κ set |
| **P3** | P1 × P2, independently multiplied | tests compounding deliberately |
| **P4** | `P × (1 + κ · mean_i[ d(p_i)·(v_i − v̄_i) ])` | combined, mean not product — bounded by κ however many preferences are stated |
| **P5** | `P + λ · Σ_i d(p_i)·(v_i − v̄_i) · medianP`, clamped ≥ 0 | bounded additive at Pursuit level; λ ∈ {0.3, 0.6, 0.9} |
| **P6** | `w_O = 0.30 + 0.20·s`, `w_R, w_F` scaled to keep the sum 1, `s` = mean \|d(p_i)\| | the one candidate that touches base weights; included because the diagnosis names the 0.30 as the attenuator |

### majorFit

| id | formula |
|---|---|
| **M0** | current — weight 0.40 inside Opportunity, no priority scaling |
| **M1** | weight 0.40 → 1.20 inside Opportunity |
| **M2** | `P × (1 + κ_m·(majorFit − m̄))`, `m̄` = pool match rate; κ_m ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.8} |
| **M3** | `P + λ_m·(majorFit − m̄)·medianP` |

M2 and M3 are independent of `academicStrengthPriority` by construction, which is the point:
"I want Engineering" and "academic strength matters to me" are different statements.

### Bad controls — must be rejected

| id | what it does |
|---|---|
| **BAD-1** | hard filter: at playing 5, drop every programme with pathway < 0.5 |
| **BAD-2** | unbounded: `P × (1 + pathway)^(2·d(playing))` |
| **BAD-3** | priority 3 moves the list: `P × (1 + 0.8·(pathway − v̄))` regardless of stated priority |
| **BAD-4** | major match as eligibility: drop every non-matching programme |

## 5. Acceptance conditions — frozen

A candidate passes only if it satisfies every condition.

### A. Direction
- **A1** playing 1→5: median `playingPathway` of top-100 entrants exceeds that of exits by **≥ 0.10**
- **A2** academic 1→5: median academic rating of entrants exceeds exits by **≥ 1.0**
- **A3** declaring a major: **≥ 80%** of matching programmes improve in rank

### B. Magnitude
- **B1** playing 1→5 top-100 churn **≥ 20** on at least three of the six men's/women's C and E
  profiles [current: 8–18]
- **B2** academic 1→5 top-100 churn **≥ 20** on at least three of those profiles [current: 3–11]
- **B3** not inert by another route: median absolute rank movement **≥ 30** for both sweeps
- **B4** churn must not exceed **60** on either sweep — above that it is displacing level rather than
  expressing a preference

### C. Hierarchy
- **C1** competitive-level churn ≥ playing churn and ≥ academic churn on **every** profile

### D. Neutral preservation
- **D1** at 3/3/3 the preference mechanism's factor is **exactly 1.0** for every programme
  (checked on the formula, not statistically)
- **D2** the 3/3/3 ranking is **identical rank-for-rank** to the control, except where a declared
  major legitimately applies

### E. Context overrides
- **E1** V3-F / W3-F: affordable substantially-below count in top 100 stays **≥ 20**
- **E2** V3-C / W3-C: playing-focused sub-below share does not fall below the control's
- **E3** V3-D / W3-D near-level share within **5pp** of control
- **E4** Candidate G positional semantics intact: measured-zero and unknown both present in the top
  100, `zero < unknown < positive` median ranks preserved

### F. No structural exclusion
- **F1** no programme may be made unrankable by any preference — every ranked programme in the
  control remains ranked
- **F2** the preference factor is strictly positive for every programme at every priority
  (formula check)

### G. Explainability
- **G1** every adjustment decomposes into base Pursuit × (1 + κ · direction · signal deviation), or
  the additive equivalent, with each term separately reportable
- **G2** monotone in the stated priority for a fixed programme (formula check)

### H. Compounding
- **H1** at 5/5/5 the **total** preference adjustment stays within **[0.5, 2.0]**
- **H2** no programme may enter the top 100 at 5/5/5 whose control rank is outside 400 **on
  preference adjustment alone** — i.e. with R, F and O all below their pool medians

## 6. Declared in advance

- Parameters are swept over the small declared sets above and selected on **conditions**, never on
  named-school outcomes. No school is named in any condition.
- If several candidates pass, the **smallest and most explainable** is recommended.
- If none passes, the phase says so and no winner is forced.
- **If the bad controls are not rejected, the phase reports the design as non-discriminating and
  stops**, as A7.17 did.
