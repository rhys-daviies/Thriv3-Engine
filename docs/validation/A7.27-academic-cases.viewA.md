# A7.27 BLIND ACADEMIC REVIEW — 15 athlete-programme cases

**This is a blind review.** Each card below is one athlete paired with one programme that the
current model already places where it says. Nothing here tells you what any proposed change
would do, what anybody thinks the right answer is, or why these particular pairings were
chosen. The codes are in an arbitrary order and mean nothing.

You are asked **one question per card**:

> From a US college recruiting perspective, how should the academic evidence affect whether
> this programme is pursued?

Pick one, and give your reasoning:

- `NO MEANINGFUL ACADEMIC CONCERN`
- `ACADEMIC CONCERN BUT STILL REASONABLE TO PURSUE`
- `SUBSTANTIAL ACADEMIC CONCERN / LOWER PURSUIT`
- `EXTREME ACADEMIC CONCERN / COACH PRE-READ REQUIRED BEFORE MEANINGFUL PURSUIT`
- `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`

`INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` is not a mild answer and is not a criticism of the programme. It means
the facts printed here do not support a judgement either way, and for some of these cards that
is the correct answer.

Two things worth holding in mind. An institution's overall acceptance rate is a fact about its
applicant pool, not a decision about this athlete. And a test-score difference is evidence, not
an admissions outcome. Recruited athletes are considered through a different process than the
general applicant pool, and how much that changes is not stated anywhere in this document.
