# A7.37 — preregistration

**Written and committed BEFORE any line of the repair was implemented and before any post-repair
number was observed.** Nothing here was chosen after seeing which option produced nicer rankings.

Production scoring baseline `0ae0399`. Diagnosis: A7.36 (`20371f4`) — class-C evidence/confidence
defect, secondary class-D semantic defect.

---

## 0. What is being repaired, in one sentence

`returningCompetition` treats **"we counted no returners"** as **"there will be no competition"**,
and those differ whenever the counting failed rather than the roster being empty.

## 1. Two facts about the architecture that bound the blast radius

Established by reading `coverage.js` and `pipeline.js` **before** designing anything, because they
decide what a confidence change can and cannot do:

1. **`combine` computes layer coverage from component WEIGHTS, never from component coverage**, and
   `grade` never enters any ranking comparison. **So changing `returningCompetition`'s grade or
   coverage cannot move a single rank.** Confidence is free; only refusals move rankings.
2. **`playingPathway` is a REQUIRED component of Opportunity.** If pathway goes unscoreable, the
   whole layer does, and the programme leaves the ranking for LIMITED_DATA. `squadRotation` does not
   depend on the entry year or on class readability, so it survives every refusal designed below —
   **pathway must therefore remain scoreable wherever rotation is**, and that is asserted as a
   failure condition, not hoped for.

This is why the repair is expressed as *refuse the competition term*, never as *penalise the value*.
`coverage.js` rule 1 — "value and coverage are never combined" — is honoured throughout: **no
shrinkage, no uncertainty multiplier, no year penalty.**

## 2. F1 — readability reaches `returningCompetition`

Two new inputs, both already computed at the call site and already consumed by the *other* layer:
`positionRows` and `unreadable` (rows at this position with no readable eligibility ceiling).

```
readable      = positionRows − unreadable
readableShare = positionRows > 0 ? readable / positionRows : 1
```

Three states are made explicit, per §3 of the brief:

| state | meaning | source |
|---|---|---|
| `KNOWN_RETURNING` | ceiling read, and it is after the entry year | `byLastSeason` |
| `KNOWN_DEPARTING` | ceiling read, and it is not after the entry year | `byLastSeason` |
| `UNKNOWN_HORIZON` | **no ceiling could be read at all** | `bucket.unreadable` — today discarded |

**Rule F1-a — the mirror of "a zero must rest on something".**
`positionRows > 0 && readable === 0` → **UNSCOREABLE**, `NO_CLASS_LABELS`, coverage 0.
This is the Saint Joseph's (ME) case and is the direct analogue of the A7.7.2 guard that
`positionalOpportunity` already carries on the departing side.

**Rule F1-b — a zero claim needs a readable majority.**
`returning === 0 && readableShare < 0.5` → **UNSCOREABLE**, `NO_CLASS_LABELS`, coverage
`readableShare`.

> **Rationale for 0.5, preregistered.** A zero is a claim about the *whole* position group. One half
> is the smallest share at which the part we read cannot be outvoted by the part we did not: below
> it, the unread rows could by themselves contain enough returners to overturn the conclusion. The
> threshold deliberately applies **only to the zero claim** — a non-zero count is affirmative
> evidence that competition exists, and refusing it would discard a real observation. This mirrors
> the A7.7.2 rule-R-b selection (MEASURED only when the whole cohort is placed) rather than
> inventing a second confidence system.

**Rule F1-c — partial unreadability is doubt, not a verdict.**
`unreadable > 0` and neither refusal fires → grade **PARTIAL**, and
`coverage = readableShare × roleCoverage`.
**The value does not move.** Unreadable players are not counted as returners (that would swap
optimistic bias for pessimistic bias, which §3 forbids) and are not counted as absent.

## 3. F2 — the horizon reaches `returningCompetition`

Two further inputs: `entryYear` (already known to the caller) and `rosterSeason` (the season the
roster index was built from).

**Rule F2-a — structural silence. THE RULE IS DERIVED, NOT CHOSEN.**

A returner needs `lastSeason > entryYear`. The largest ceiling any row in a roster of season `S` can
carry is a property of `S` and the division's eligibility model, obtained by asking
`eligibilityCeiling` itself rather than by hardcoding:

```
maxAttainableLastSeason(S, division) = max over {class × redshirt} of eligibilityCeiling(...).lastSeason
```

For `S = 2026`, computed before implementation:

| division | model | max attainable | first entry year at which a returner is impossible |
|---|---|---|---|
| NCAA D1, D2 | 5-year | **2030** | **2030** |
| NCAA D3, NAIA | 4 seasons | **2029** | **2029** |
| NJCAA, USCAA | no rule | — | already refused by the existing `eligibilityRuled` guard |

**`entryYear >= maxAttainableLastSeason` → UNSCOREABLE**, coverage 0. At such a horizon a zero is
forced by the calendar for every programme alike, so it measures nothing about any of them.

> This predicts, before it is run, that F2-a fires **nowhere at 2027 or 2028**, for **D3 and NAIA
> only at 2029**, and **everywhere at 2030**. A7.36 measured 60.8% of men's cells at the maximum at
> 2029; D3 (1,244) + NAIA (723) is 1,967 of 3,468 scoreable cells ≈ 57%. **If the repair is right,
> those two numbers should line up.** Recorded here so the check cannot be constructed afterwards.

**Rule F2-b — depth degrades authority, never value.**

```
depth = entryYear − rosterSeason;   MEASURED requires depth <= 1, otherwise PARTIAL
```

> **Rationale for 1, preregistered.** At depth `d` the entry-year squad contains roughly `d ×
> (first-year share)` players not yet recruited and therefore invisible to this roster. `pool.js`
> records the men's first-year share as **29.5%**. So ≈30% of the entry-year squad is unobserved at
> depth 1 and ≈59% at depth 2 — **depth 2 is where the majority of the thing being estimated is not
> in the data**, and a MEASURED grade must not survive that. A7.8.1 independently found the median
> placeable share of returners falls to zero at a two-year horizon.

**No numerical year penalty is applied anywhere.** F2 changes only grade, coverage and refusal.

## 4. Evidence-state rules, consolidated

Evaluated in order; the first that matches wins.

| # | condition | result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | no roster for the programme | UNSCOREABLE `NO_ROSTER_ON_FILE` *(unchanged)* |
| 2 | no eligibility rule for the division | UNSCOREABLE `NO_CLASS_LABELS` *(unchanged)* |
| 3 | **F2-a** `entryYear >= maxAttainableLastSeason` | **UNSCOREABLE `NO_CLASS_LABELS`**, coverage 0 |
| 4 | **F1-a** rows present, none readable | **UNSCOREABLE `NO_CLASS_LABELS`**, coverage 0 |
| 5 | **F1-b** `returning === 0` and `readableShare < 0.5` | **UNSCOREABLE `NO_CLASS_LABELS`**, coverage `readableShare` |
| 6 | otherwise | SCOREABLE, value unchanged; **MEASURED** iff `unknown === 0` **and** `readableShare === 1` **and** `depth <= 1`; else **PARTIAL**. `coverage = readableShare × roleCoverage` |

`NO_CLASS_LABELS` is reused deliberately rather than adding vocabulary: in every new refusal the
missing input really is a readable class year, either because it was absent or because the entry
year has outrun what a class year can tell us.

## 5. Single-component behaviour — audited, not assumed

**Rotation-only pathway is semantically legitimate and will be permitted.** `squadRotation` measures
how widely a programme has historically shared minutes at a position. That is a true statement about
playing opportunity which does not depend on the entry year, so it remains meaningful exactly where
the competition term stops being meaningful.

It must never be presentable as a full pathway. Therefore:

- pathway remains scoreable from rotation alone;
- its grade is **PARTIAL always** — never MEASURED when only one half was used;
- `basis.rotationOnly` is set, and `basis.competitionRefusedBecause` carries the refusal reason so an
  explanation can say *which* thing is unknown;
- the 0.6 / 0.4 value formula is **unchanged** when both halves score.

## 6. F0 — coverage reporting

`playingPathway` reports `coverage = competitionShare` for **both** single-half cases. Correct for
competition-only (0.6), wrong for rotation-only, which contributes 0.4. Corrected to the weight of
the half actually used. **Reporting only** — no weight, no value, no threshold changes.

## 7. Success conditions — frozen

| id | condition |
|---|---|
| **S1** | Saint Joseph's (ME) FORWARD: competition is UNSCOREABLE. Its pathway is no longer `1.000 MEASURED` |
| **S2** | matched completeness: the less-complete record no longer wins systematically. **Wins between 40% and 60% of comparable strata**, or the comparable population collapses because those cells now refuse — **both count as success and must be reported distinctly** |
| **S3** | a fully-readable group with 1 returner still scores above a fully-readable group with 8, and is MEASURED at depth ≤ 1 |
| **S4** | at entry 2030: **0%** of cells at competition `1.000 MEASURED`, **and** the pathway value distribution is not a constant — interdecile range `p90 − p10 >= 0.20` |
| **S5** | no inverse bias: median pathway over **fully-readable, depth ≤ 1** cells moves by `<= 0.02` |
| **S6** | no explanation asserts "few returning players" where the competition term refused |

## 8. Failure conditions — frozen

| id | condition |
|---|---|
| **X1** | entry 2030 remains universally `1.000`, at **any** grade — relabelling MEASURED as PARTIAL is not a repair |
| **X2** | any programme becomes LIMITED_DATA solely because the competition term refused |
| **X3** | either V1 snapshot moves |
| **X4** | Candidate G, Recruitability, Financial, majorFit, academic admissions, initial or continuing eligibility change in any respect |
| **X5** | the repair makes the *more*-complete record win systematically (S2 inverted) |

## 9. Distributions measured — fixed before the repair exists

Men's and women's separately, at entry years **2027, 2028, 2029, 2030**: value percentiles,
MEASURED / PARTIAL / UNSCOREABLE shares, rotation-only share, both-components share, maximum-value
share. Plus: the A7.36 §5 matched analysis (wins / losses / ties / median difference / effect size),
the §9 athlete classifications for V3-B/C/D and W3-B/C/D, the playing-priority 1→5 sweep, and
top-100 programme strength **as a regression guard only**.

## 10. Prohibited in this phase

The 0.6 / 0.4 value weights · Pursuit weights · the Opportunity weight · gates · turnover in any
form · any penalty applied to weak programmes · moving positional need into Opportunity · Candidate
G · Financial · level preference · playing-priority strength · the value formula itself · the
`typicalStarters` denominator · any new REASON vocabulary.

## 11. Declared in advance

- Acceptance rests on §7 and §8 **only**. It does **not** rest on destination backtest, on top-100
  programme strength, or on the rankings looking better.
- If F1 and F2 disagree about a cell, the **refusal wins** — the component is being taught to say "I
  do not know", and a rule that says so outranks one that says a number.
- If the measurements fall between the success and failure conditions, the verdict is
  **REQUIRES TOURNAMENT**, and no variant is selected inside A7.37.
