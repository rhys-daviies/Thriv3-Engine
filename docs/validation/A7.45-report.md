# A7.45 — Playing Pathway fallback semantics audit

Read-only audit of behaviour that **predates A7.44**, run on the frozen A7.44
roster snapshot (digest `1e36644a…`, 62,251 rows, entry year 2027). No scoring
was changed to produce it. Institutions, positions and counts only.

## Policy A — what the current fallback does

Playing Pathway is `0.60 × competition + 0.40 × rotation`. When **competition**
refuses and **rotation** survives, A7.37 lets the pathway continue from rotation
alone, reporting coverage 0.4 instead of 1.0.

## What the two halves measure

| | **competition** (`returningCompetition`) | **rotation** (`squadRotation`) |
|---|---|---|
| source | 2026 `roster_players` + eligibility ceilings | `roster_players` **2022–2025** `minutes_played` |
| transform | returners past the entry year, against `typicalStarters` | inverse-Herfindahl `effectivePlayers / groupSize`, placed on the sport-position p10–p90 range |
| specificity | this programme, this position, **this entry year** | this position, but programme → **division → sport** fallback |
| answers | *who will still be here when the athlete arrives* | *how widely this programme has historically shared minutes here* |
| refuses when | no bucket, no eligibility rule, no readable horizon, or (A7.44) unreadable positions | only if the programme has no 2026 roster at all, or no norm exists |

`squadRotation`'s own design comment states the limit directly: *"A PROGRAMME
TRAIT, AND ONLY THAT… it knows nothing about who will be on the roster when they
arrive. That is why it is half of Playing Pathway and not the whole of it"* — and
gives the A7.8 counterexample of a programme with nine midfielders remaining
scoring above one with seven, on three-year-old rotation.

## Three findings

**1. The reduced coverage does not reach the layer.** `coverage.combine` derives
layer coverage from component **weights**, not from component coverage. Measured
on identical inputs:

```
pathway BOTH      value 0.800  coverage 1.0  grade MEASURED
pathway ROT-ONLY  value 0.800  coverage 0.4  grade PARTIAL
Opportunity BOTH  0.6950  coverage 1   │   ROT-ONLY  0.6950  coverage 1
```

The 0.4 is reported and discarded. A rotation-only pathway enters Athlete
Opportunity at its full **0.65 share** with the same authority as a fully
evidenced one. Only the grade differs, and grade never enters a ranking.

**2. The halves are close to independent.** Pearson r(competition, rotation) =
**+0.174** across the 7,837 cells where both are scoreable (GK +0.140, DEF
+0.136, MID +0.187, FWD +0.163). Rotation explains about 3% of competition's
variance, so it is not a proxy for it.

*(The `derivePlayingNorms` header's "r = 0.061" is a different pair —
`positionalOpportunity` against playing share — so this is a new measurement,
not a reproduction of that one.)*

**3. Less evidence produces more extreme claims.** Pathway value in the fallback
cells against the blended cells:

| | n | sd | ≤0.05 | ≥0.95 | exactly 0 | exactly 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| both halves | 7,837 | 0.136 | 0 | 12 (0.2%) | 0 | 4 |
| rotation only | 403 | **0.223** | 12 (3.0%) | 16 (4.0%) | **8** | **10** |

The blended measure cannot reach 0.000 at all. Thirteen programmes now assert the
minimum possible Playing Pathway on a 2022–25 rotation statistic.

## Policy simulation

Diagnostic only; no policy was implemented. Measured against the pre-A7.44
baseline on one frozen corpus (men's pool 1,205 active, women's 1,422 — grown
from 1,159/1,240 by Phase 7D/7E, so absolute ranks are not comparable to the
A7.44 report's).

| policy | pathway scoreable / refused | LIMITED_DATA | moved | median | **max** | T25 | T50 | T100 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **A** current | 8,240 / 0 | 2,709 | 6,447 | 4 | **508** | 7 | 11 | 22 |
| **B** pathway refuses | 7,837 / **403** | 3,025 | 6,528 | 23 | **63** | 7 | 15 | 38 |
| **C** rotation at 0.40 | 8,240 / 0 | 2,709 | 6,784 | 8 | **698** | 7 | 15 | 34 |

Fallback-cell value: A median 0.489 (0.000–1.000) · C median 0.195, capped at
0.400 · B none.

## Why Policy C is rejected

C treats the unmeasured competition contribution as a zero. `coverage.js` names
that defect in its own header — *"dividing by the applicable weight would treat
an unmeasured component as a zero, and that is the neutral-prior defect wearing
the opposite sign."* A fallback cell can never exceed 0.400 however strong the
programme, which converts absence of evidence into a low score, and it produces
the **largest** displacement of the three (698).

## Why Policy B is selected for testing

Because it is the only policy whose ranking authority matches its evidence:
the stated 0.65 weight is currently applied to a half the design documents as
insufficient alone; the 0.4 coverage meant to restrain it provably does not
reach the layer; and 42% of those cells do not rest on the programme's own
history.

**This selection is made on evidence semantics, not on ranking stability.** B is
not the most stable option on every measure — it has more Top-100 churn (38 vs
22) and a higher median movement (23 vs 4) than the current policy, and it moves
325 more programmes into LIMITED_DATA. It is chosen despite that, because the
alternative is a rank-bearing score that asserts more than the evidence supports.

Note for whoever implements it: 133 of the 403 fallbacks predate A7.44, so
Policy B changes existing behaviour and is not only a response to A7.44.
