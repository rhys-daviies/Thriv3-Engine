## The major, chosen before any ranking existed

`majorFit` has weight 1.2 against Playing Pathway's 0.65 — when a major is
declared it is the **largest single component of Opportunity**, taking a 0.545
share. A8.0 never exercised it, because no reference athlete declares a major.

The major was selected on the distribution of `colleges.notable_majors` alone —
programme evidence, never a ranking — against four criteria fixed in advance.
**Kinesiology**, entered as the free text `'exercise science'` so the synonym
mapping is exercised as real intake data would exercise it.

| criterion | Kinesiology | why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| both branches carry mass | men's 457 / 451 / 9 · women's 617 / 604 / 8 | the most balanced of the 14 families in **both** sports. Business (92.8%) or Mathematics (7.9%) would make one branch a rounding error |
| non-empty third bucket | 9 men's, 8 women's with no major evidence | `NO_PROGRAMME_MAJOR_EVIDENCE` is exercised, not hypothetical |
| smallest division skew | 25.6 points (D1 36.6% → NAIA 62.2%) | next best, Art & Design, spreads 32.0. No family is even; provision genuinely differs by institution type |
| **skew runs against the incumbent** | rarest at **Division I** | D1 holds 25 of 25 in A8.0's top 25. A declared Kinesiology major pushes *against* the schools already at the top, so the choice cannot flatter the ranking |

The fourth criterion is the one that matters: the major was picked to be
awkward, not favourable.

## Preregistered acceptance tests

Defined before results were inspected; implemented in
`server/scripts/a8Extension.test.js`.

The central requirement: **changing only `intended_major` must not change
Recruitability, Financial, Playing Pathway, programme trajectory, or any
unrelated evidence state.** It may change majorFit and, through it, Opportunity
and Pursuit — and only by that route. Success is *not* defined as any school
rising.

| | asserts |
|---|---|
| E0 | the A8.0 artifact is byte-for-byte the one that was frozen |
| E1 | A–H and the two A8.0 validation athletes unchanged, pinned by hash |
| E2 | the coverage fixture is declared synthetic and carries no person |
| E3 | no person-level field reached the extension |
| E4 | every arm differs from its base only in the fields it declares |
| E5 | the major counterfactual differs **only** in `intended_major` |
| E6 | the financial arms differ **only** in financial fields |
| E7 | the entry-year arms differ **only** in `recruiting_class_year` |
| M1 | a declared major exercises match, non-match and no-evidence |
| M2 | an undeclared major is NOT_APPLICABLE, never a score of zero |
| M3 | declaring a major changes Recruitability and Financial in **no** cell |
| M4 | it does move Opportunity and Pursuit, through the component that owns it |
| F1 | an unanswered contribution refuses; it never becomes zero |
| F2 | NOT_A_CONSTRAINT is the absence of a limit, not a large number |
| F3 | a stated contribution prices the pool; a larger one binds less often |
| F4 | changing the contribution changes Recruitability and Opportunity in **no** cell |
| H1 | MEASURED is reachable at depth 1 and at no greater depth |
| H2 | programmeTrajectory cannot read the entry year, and does not move |
| H3 | changing the entry year changes Financial in **no** cell |

S0–S15 from A8.0 also run against the extension: `a8Baseline.test.js` is
parameterised over both artifacts rather than restated, so the extension gets
the identical sixteen checks and they cannot drift apart.

**Shown to fail.** Seven deliberately broken artifacts, each caught by its own
test. One of them caught a defect in the tests rather than the engine: an
earlier F1 was scoped to the supported universe and did **not** fail when a
zero was planted in an unsupported cell. Financial reads a cost and a
contribution and never an eligibility rule, so F1 now covers every cell.

not a large invented budget. No answer refuses rather than scoring zero.

### A product consequence, recorded rather than fixed

Financial is a **required** layer in Pursuit, so an athlete who has not stated
a contribution gets **no ranking at all** — 0 ranked, 1,422 LIMITED_DATA. That
is the frozen semantics behaving correctly: it refuses rather than inventing a
budget, and UNKNOWN has not become ZERO. It is also a product-level
consequence that whoever integrates V2 must decide about, since V1 ranks such
an athlete today. No user is affected now — V2 is wired to no route — and
nothing was changed here.

## §F — the A7.37 horizon boundary

`depth = entryYear − rosterSeason`, roster season 2026, `MEASURED_HORIZON_DEPTH
= 1`. **Every frozen athlete enters in 2028 — depth 2** — so A8.0 could only
ever observe the PARTIAL side of this rule.

| arm | depth | Playing Pathway MEASURED | PARTIAL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | 1 | **34** | 799 |
| 2028 | 2 | **0** | 832 |
| 2029 | 3 | **0** | 399 |

MEASURED is reachable at depth 1 and nowhere deeper — the rule, observed from
both sides for the first time.

| | 2027 vs 2028 | 2029 vs 2028 |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial changed** | **0** | **0** |
| **programmeTrajectory changed** | **0** of 814 | **0** of 396 |
| Opportunity changed | 712 | 764 |

Recruitability is *not* asserted unchanged here, and must not be: it reads
`entryYear` legitimately, through the arrivals horizon. Claiming otherwise
would be asserting something false about the engine.

The roster evidence itself cannot move between arms — the pool context is built
once per sport and shared by every arm in the run, so all three read the same
object.

At 2029 only 394 programmes remain rankable, against 816 at 2028: by then most
of the roster on file has exhausted its eligibility, so position groups empty
out and `NO_PLAYERS_AT_POSITION` fires. Truthful refusal, not a defect.

## §G — the extension artifact

`docs/validation/A8.0B-extension.json` — 13,569 cells, 10 arms, 4.50 MB,
deterministic across three rebuilds.

Components are captured **per arm family**, not per run: MAJOR carries
`majorFit`, ENTRY_YEAR carries `playingPathway` and `programmeTrajectory`, and
FINANCIAL carries none, because its whole question is answered by the Financial
layer cell every artifact already holds. Capturing all three everywhere cost
1.9 MB describing cells nothing asks about.
