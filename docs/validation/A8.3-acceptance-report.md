## §P Engine vs product

**ENGINE issues: none.** No structural blocker, no evidence-truthfulness
blocker, no REVIEW_REQUIRED scoring anomaly, no systematic ranking pathology.

**PRODUCT integration requirements:**

1. A resolved family contribution is required before rankings are presented —
   Financial is required in Pursuit, so an undeclared family gets 0 ranked.
2. Unsupported associations need a truthful state: "no eligibility rule on
   file", never a low score and never silent omission.
3. LIMITED_DATA must read as "not enough evidence", never "bad match".
4. Retain the full universe; Top 100 is the operational slice, not the model.
5. Present bands or regions, not adjacent ranks — 600–975 adjacent pairs per
   athlete differ by less than 0.001, so #100 vs #101 is not a defensible
   difference.
6. Major evidence is **positive-only**. A match raises a programme; absence
   says nothing. The UI must not render an unknown as a negative, and should
   say that a non-matching programme may still offer the major.
7. **New:** a declared major materially reshapes the list through legitimate
   positive evidence — 569 of 1,229 programmes. Users should be able to see
   that the major is influencing the ranking and to remove it.

**DATA expansion (not blockers):** 481 unsupported programmes awaiting
researched eligibility rules; 90 four-year programmes without a 2026 roster;
supported-universe source gaps above; and an exhaustive major catalogue, which
would let majorFit answer the 539 cells it now refuses and would make an
authoritative-negative branch legitimate.
