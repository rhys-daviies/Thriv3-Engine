# A7.37 — Playing Pathway evidence repair

**Preregistration: `59905cb`** — written and committed before any implementation and before any
post-repair number existed. Production scoring baseline `0ae0399`.

# VERDICT: **REQUIRES TOURNAMENT**

Five of the six acceptance criteria are met, two of them decisively. The sixth — **no inverse
completeness bias** — is **not** met, and the preregistration's own rule (§11) forbids selecting a
variant inside A7.37 when the result lands between the success and failure conditions.

The repair is **not** rejected: it removes the false certainty and the horizon collapse exactly as
designed, and the residual bias is **not caused by the new rules**. It is the pre-existing
renormalisation convention becoming visible once the competition term starts refusing — a different
defect, in a place A7.37 was forbidden to touch.

## 3. F1 — readability reaches the component

`positionRows` and `unreadable` are now passed in. Both already existed at the call site and were
already being given to `positionalOpportunity`; only the returning side was blind.

```
readable = positionRows − unreadable;   readableShare = readable / positionRows
```

- **F1-a** rows present, none readable → **UNSCOREABLE**. The mirror of the A7.7.2 guard.
- **F1-b** `returning === 0` and `readableShare < 0.5` → **UNSCOREABLE**.
- **F1-c** otherwise `unreadable > 0` → grade **PARTIAL**, `coverage = readableShare × roleCoverage`.

**The value never moves.** Unreadable players are not counted as returners and not counted as absent
— §3 of the brief forbids swapping optimistic bias for pessimistic, and `coverage.js` rule 1 forbids
combining value with coverage. The three states are named (`KNOWN_RETURNING`, `KNOWN_DEPARTING`,
`UNKNOWN_HORIZON`) and the counts are reported in the basis.

## 4. F2 — the horizon reaches the component

**The rule is derived, not chosen.** A returner needs `lastSeason > entryYear`; the largest ceiling a
roster row can carry is obtained by asking `eligibilityCeiling` itself across every class and both
redshirt states:

| division | max attainable from a 2026 roster |
|---|---|
| NCAA D1, D2 | **2030** |
| NCAA D3, NAIA | **2029** |

`entryYear >= maxAttainableLastSeason` → **UNSCOREABLE**: at that horizon the zero is forced by the
calendar for every programme alike.

**F2-b** `depth = entryYear − rosterSeason`; MEASURED requires `depth <= 1`. **No year penalty
anywhere** — depth changes grade only.

> **The preregistration made a falsifiable prediction and it held.** It predicted the rule would fire
> nowhere at 2027–28, for D3 and NAIA only at 2029, and everywhere at 2030 — and that the 2029 case
> should account for roughly the 60.8% A7.36 measured, since D3 + NAIA ≈ 57% of scoreable men's
> cells. **Measured: 54.1% rotation-only at 2029, 100% at 2030, 0% at 2027–28.**

## 6. Single-component behaviour — audited

**Rotation-only is legitimate and is now labelled.** Rotation measures how widely a programme has
historically shared minutes; that is true independently of the entry year, so it remains meaningful
exactly where competition stops being so. It may carry the value and must not carry the certainty:

- grade is **PARTIAL always** when one half is used, whatever that half's own grade;
- `basis.rotationOnly` and `basis.competitionRefusedBecause` are set;
- the 0.6 / 0.4 formula is untouched when both halves score.

**Blast-radius invariant, asserted as a test:** `playingPathway` is a REQUIRED component of
Opportunity, so a refusal reaching pathway itself would drop programmes into LIMITED_DATA. Rotation
survives every refusal introduced here, so **no programme leaves the ranking**. Confirmed across all
four entry years.

## 8. Matched completeness — **the criterion that failed**

Division × position × rotation quintile, entry 2028, identical data:

| | strata | less-complete wins | losses | ties | win rate | median Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **men's before** | 26 | **26** | 0 | 0 | **100.0%** | **+0.233** |
| **men's after** | 26 | 4 | 22 | 0 | **15.4%** | **−0.096** |
| **women's before** | 28 | **28** | 0 | 0 | **100.0%** | **+0.234** |
| **women's after** | 28 | 4 | 24 | 0 | **14.3%** | **−0.084** |

**Effect size:** the bias magnitude falls by **2.4× (men's)** and **2.8× (women's)**, and **changes
sign**. Preregistered success was a 40–60% win rate. **15.4% and 14.3% miss it, and failure
condition X5 fires.**

### Why — and why it is not the new rules' fault

When the competition half refuses, `playingPathway` renormalises onto rotation alone. Since
competition's median (0.600) sits above rotation's (0.437 men's, 0.515 women's), **any** cell losing
its competition half drops by approximately `competitionShare × (median rotation − median
competition)`:

| | predicted | measured |
|---|---|---|
| men's | **−0.098** | **−0.096** |
| women's | −0.051 | −0.084 |

**The men's figure is reproduced almost exactly; the women's accounts for about 60% of it.** This is
the renormalisation convention `coverage.js` defends by name — *"the alternative … would treat an
unmeasured component as a zero, and that is the neutral-prior defect wearing the opposite sign"* —
and it means a missing half is implicitly imputed as equal to the surviving half. That convention
predates A7.37 and sits outside its frozen scope. **A7.37 did not create this; it made it visible by
being the first change to make the competition term refuse at scale.**

**One bug this caught in my own work.** I wrote the horizon guard with a bare `Number.isFinite`.

it.

## 19. Recommended next phase

**A7.38 — the single-half fallback tournament.** Preregister and compare, on the matched-completeness
and horizon measurements already frozen here:

1. **keep A7.37 as-is** — accept a −0.09 bias in exchange for removing a +0.23 one;
2. **anchor the surviving half** — rescale rotation onto competition's location before it stands in,
   so dropping a half does not move the level;
3. **carry the missing half at the pool median** — an explicit, declared prior rather than an implicit
   one, which `coverage.js` currently forbids and would have to be argued;
4. **refuse the pathway outright** when competition refuses — clean semantics, and it would drop
   programmes into LIMITED_DATA, so it must be measured before it is considered.

held as regressions.
