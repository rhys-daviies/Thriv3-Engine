# A7.22 — preregistration

**Written and committed before any candidate was measured.** Corrects the two conditions A7.21
identified as faulty. Nothing in this phase changes production.

---

## 1. Meaningful levelFactor spread — the definition, and why

The level anchor is `P ← P · f(a)` where `f(a) = 1 − τ(p)·(1 − a)` and `a = align(δ)`.

**A multiplier that is constant across the pool cannot reorder anything.** Two programmes swap
places under a multiplier only if the ratio of their multipliers crosses the ratio of their
pre-multiplier priorities. So the reordering power of a level sweep is carried entirely by the
*spread of the log multiplier*, not by τ and not by the level of `f`.

For the sweep 1→5 the multiplicative change applied to a programme is

```
ρ(a) = f₅(a) / f₁(a) = (1 − τ₅·(1 − a)) / (1 − τ₁·(1 − a))      τ₅ = 0.50, τ₁ = 0.10
```

and the sweep's total reordering range over the pool is

```
levelSpread = ln( ρ(a_p90) / ρ(a_p10) )
```

measured over the ranked universe, interdecile so a handful of outliers cannot manufacture it.
`levelSpread → 0` means the sweep multiplies every programme by the same number and **must**
produce zero reordering, whatever τ is.

**The threshold is relative to the candidate under test, which is the only non-arbitrary choice
available.** A hierarchy condition asks "did level move more than playing?". That question is only
answerable if level had *at least as much room to move*. So:

```
hierarchy is INFORMATIVE for a given candidate  iff  levelSpread ≥ prefSpread
prefSpread = ln( adj_p90 / adj_p10 ) of that candidate's own preference adjustment, same pool
```

Both sides are interdecile log-ranges of a multiplier on the same quantity, so they are directly
comparable and carry the same units. The criterion is mechanical, applies to every profile
identically, and **names no profile**. The absolute `levelSpread` is reported for every profile
alongside the verdict so the reader can see where the line fell.

Where the criterion is not met the hierarchy is reported **NOT INFORMATIVE**, never FAIL.

## 2. The 60-churn cap is removed

Replaced by the behavioural safeguards in §4. Raw churn is reported, never a rejection threshold.
The 25–35 region is retained as intuition only. Any profile moving more than 50 is **diagnosed**,
not rejected.

## 3. Candidates

### Track A — P4, the bounded-combined architecture

```
direction(p) = (p − 3) / 2
adj = 1 + κ · mean over STATED preferences of [ direction(pᵢ) · (vᵢ − v̄ᵢ) ]
P' = P · adj
```

`κ` swept over **{0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8, 1.2}** — five values below A7.21's 0.8 to
resolve the response curve, and one above as a stress point. Frozen before results.

**Pool reference `v̄`**, three variants, all frozen:

| id | `v̄` | rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **REF-MED** | pool median of the signal | A7.21's choice; ranking is inherently relative |
| **REF-MID** | fixed 0.5 | both `playingPathway` and `academicStrengthFit` are constructed on [0, 1], so 0.5 is a defensible semantic midpoint that cannot drift with pool composition |
| **REF-MEAN** | pool mean | stable under reordering, sensitive to skew; included to separate "median vs mean" from "pool-relative vs fixed" |

**Stability test.** Each reference is re-measured on three deterministic pool perturbations — drop
all NCAA D3, drop the weakest strength quartile, drop every programme with no roster on file — and
the change in each programme's preference adjustment is reported. A reference is **unstable** if
the median absolute change in `adj` exceeds **0.05** under any perturbation.

### Track B — majorFit, decided independently of Track A

`majorFit` Opportunity weight swept over **{0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 1.20, 1.60, 2.40}**. 2.40 is the
stress point. No new architecture; no coupling to `academicStrengthPriority`.

### Bad controls — retained

BAD-1 hard preference filter · BAD-2 unbounded adjustment · BAD-3 priority 3 moves the list ·
BAD-4 major as eligibility · **BAD-5 runaway stacking** (independent per-preference multipliers at
κ=2.4, the A7.21 P3 shape).

## 4. Acceptance conditions — frozen

### A. Direction
- **A1** playing 1→5: median `playingPathway` of top-100 entrants exceeds exits by **≥ 0.10**
- **A2** academic 1→5: median academic rating of entrants exceeds exits by **≥ 1.0**
- **A3** monotone: top-100 churn versus the 3/3/3 list is non-decreasing across priorities
  3 → 4 → 5 and 3 → 2 → 1, on every profile

### B. Magnitude
- **B1** playing 1→5 top-100 churn **≥ 20** on at least three of the six C/E/B profiles
- **B2** academic 1→5 likewise
- **B3** median absolute rank movement **≥ 30** for both sweeps

### C. Hierarchy — corrected
- **C1** on every profile where `levelSpread ≥ prefSpread`, level churn ≥ playing churn **and**
  ≥ academic churn. Profiles failing the spread test are reported NOT INFORMATIVE and excluded.
- **C2** at least **half** the twelve profiles must be informative, or the hierarchy result is
  reported as untestable for that candidate.

### D. Neutral
- **D1** at 3/3/3 the adjustment is exactly 1.0 for every programme (formula check)
- **D2** the 3/3/3 ranking is identical rank-for-rank to the control

### E. Preservation
- **E1** V3-F / W3-F affordable substantially-below count in top 100 **≥ 20**
- **E2** V3-C / W3-C sub-below share not below control
- **E3** V3-D / W3-D near-level share within **5pp** of control
- **E4** Candidate G positional semantics: measured-zero and unknown both present in the top 100,
  `zero < unknown < positive` median ranks preserved
- **E5** V1 snapshots 0 differences; A7.18 A/B/F/D baseline reproducible; no scorer file changed

### F. Safety — replacing the churn cap
- **F1** no programme made unrankable by any preference
- **F2** adjustment strictly positive for every programme at every priority (formula check)
- **F3** **bounded**: total adjustment within **[0.5, 2.0]** at every priority combination tested,
  on every profile
- **F4** **no preference-only flooding**: no programme may enter a top 100 whose R, F and O are all
  below their pool medians and whose control rank is outside 400
- **F5** no negative Pursuit anywhere

## 5. Declared in advance

- Parameters are selected on conditions, never on named-school outcomes. No school appears in any
  condition.
- If several κ pass, the **smallest** that satisfies B is recommended, not the largest-moving.
- Track A and Track B are decided **separately**. Either may be recommended without the other.
- If the bad controls are accepted, the phase reports the design as non-discriminating and stops.
- If nothing passes, none is recommended.
