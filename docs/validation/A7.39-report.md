## 2. The finding that reframes the phase

> **"Unreadable" has been used to mean "readClassYear failed", and it does not mean that.**

A row produces no eligibility ceiling when any of three different things is true:

| | cause | men's | women's | is it a parser problem? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the label is **absent** | 386 | 366 | **no** — nothing to parse |
| 2 | the label parses and carries **no class** | 47 | 5 | **no** — a bare redshirt marker is not a year |
| 3 | the **division** has no eligibility rule | 0 | 19 | **no** — the label read correctly |
| | **rows where the parser failed to recognise a label** | **0** | **0** | — |

**Zero rows, out of 117 distinct labels across 62,257 roster rows.** There is no mapping to add,
and adding one would have to invent a meaning that no label carries.

## 6. The one accuracy question found — and deliberately left open

Auditing the 117 labels the parser *accepts* surfaced something the coverage audit would have missed:
**suffix redshirt forms are not flagged as redshirts.**

`"Jr.-R"` reads as JUNIOR with `redshirt: false`, while `"R-Jr."` reads as JUNIOR with
`redshirt: true`. The `REDSHIRT` pattern is prefix-anchored. 17 rows in 2026, 44 across all seasons
(Hawaii Hilo, Nebraska at Kearney, San Francisco State).

At other programmes the same suffix slot carries `-TR` and `-1L`/`-2L`/`-3L` (Lamar), so `-R` is
**plausibly** Redshirt. `hiloathletics.com` publishes **no legend, key or expanded form**. A page
summary offered "almost certainly Redshirt, based on context clues" — **that is an inference, not a
source**, and §5 and §6 both forbid acting on it.

**Left unchanged, and both alternatives are worse:**

- treating the whole label as unreadable discards a class year nobody doubts;
- asserting a redshirt hands the athlete an extra year of eligibility on a guess.

Recorded in the artifact under `requiresResearch` and **pinned by a test that says the current
reading may be wrong**, so a future change is a decision made on evidence rather than a silent drift.

redshirt, no transfer and no repeated year.
