## 1–2. Candidates frozen

The preregistration argued the space is **closed** rather than sampled: with the 0.6 / 0.4 blend

## 3. Anchor and prior rationale

**B's anchor = 0.5**, frozen: both components are *constructed* on [0,1] — competition as
`1/(1+pressure/0.75)`, rotation as a clamped p10→p90 placement — so 0.5 is the midpoint of the scale
rather than a pool statistic, and cannot carry sex, division, position or strength. **C1** is the pool
median of the missing component per sport; **C2** the same per sport and position.

Two of these are fatal on their own, and both are named prohibitions in the brief:

- **The rotation prior encodes programme strength.** Men's median runs **0.553 → 0.437** from the

- **The competition prior moves with the entry horizon**, 0.466 → 0.714 across 2027–2029. The prior
  meant to stand in for missing evidence is itself a function of the very horizon A7.37 just
  repaired, so C1 would reintroduce horizon dependence through the back door.

**C1 therefore fails §6 and §16 on semantics.** The preregistration anticipated this exact outcome:
*"Test whether such a prior is actually defensible. If not, reject C rather than inventing one."*

## 10. Coverage, preference and strength (R5, R6, R8)

Coverage is in §5 and §6. **D's cost is the decisive one:** it removes **65% of the men's universe at
2029** and **100% at 2030**, and at 2030 no athlete could be given a ranking at all, because pathway
is a REQUIRED component of Opportunity. The brief asked that D not be rejected merely for falling
coverage — it is rejected for reaching **zero**, which is not a coverage cost but the absence of a
product.

**Preference (R6) and strength (R8) were not measured per candidate**, and that is a deliberate scope
call rather than an omission: A, B, C1, C2 and D are each eliminated on §4, §5 or §6 by
universe-level properties of the pathway values themselves, and no ranking run can reverse an
elimination on those grounds. **A's figures stand from A7.37** (priority 1→5 churn 14 men's / 18
women's; top-100 median strength unchanged to within 0.1), and A is what production keeps.

## 13. Why there is no winner, stated as a single finding

| cand | §4 completeness | §6 prior | R1 horizon | R5 coverage | R2 truthfulness |

| **A** | **FAIL** −0.096 | n/a | PASS | PASS | **best** |

| **D** | **N/A** | n/a | — | **FAIL** 0% at 2030 | discards evidence |

> **The tournament's real result is that the fallback cannot be fixed by choosing a better fallback.**
> The three ways to fill a missing half each fail for the same underlying reason in different
> clothing: **there is no quantity available that is both informative about the missing component and
> free of the things it correlates with.** A fixed anchor is uninformative and barely moves the bias;
> an empirical prior is informative *because* it carries strength, division and horizon; and refusal
> removes the product. Renormalisation's −0.09 is the price of not inventing any of them.
