reasonably say a school is worth pursuing and still say it does not make the first hundred.

## How to answer

For each programme, tick one decision and one first-100 answer.

- **PURSUE STRONGLY** — on the first outreach list, this week.
- **PURSUE** — worth contacting.
- **BORDERLINE** — depends on something you would need to check or ask.
- **LOW PRIORITY** — would contact only after the others.
- **WOULD NOT PURSUE** — would not email this school for this athlete.
- **INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION** — you cannot say. **This is not a mild answer and it is not a
  criticism of the school.** It means the facts printed here do not support a decision either
  way. Some of these programmes genuinely have very little on file, and saying so is the
  correct answer for them.

Then: _If you were building this athlete's outreach list today, should this school be inside roughly the first 100 programmes contacted?_ — **YES / NO / UNSURE**.

Do not try to guess a rank. UNSURE is a real answer.
