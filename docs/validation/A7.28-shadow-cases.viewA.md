# A7.28 BLIND REVIEW — should a pre-read be checked first?

**This is a blind review.** Each card is one athlete paired with one programme that the current
model already places where it says. Nothing here tells you what any assessment concluded about
any card, or why these pairings were chosen. The codes are in an arbitrary order.

One question per card:

> Based only on the available recruiting and admissions evidence, should Thriv3 recommend
> checking academic admissibility with the coach — or requesting an academic pre-read — before
> treating this programme as straightforwardly recruitable?

Answer **YES**, **NO**, or **UNSURE / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**, with a brief reason.

You are **not** being asked how likely admission is, and you should not estimate one. The
question is only whether the evidence on the card is enough to warrant asking the coach before
Thriv3 treats the programme as a straightforward target.

Worth holding in mind: an institution's acceptance rate describes its applicant pool, not a
decision about this athlete; a test-score difference is evidence, not an outcome; and recruited
athletes are considered through a different process from the general pool, by an amount that is
not stated anywhere in this document and differs by institution.

**UNSURE** is a real answer and is not a criticism of the programme.
