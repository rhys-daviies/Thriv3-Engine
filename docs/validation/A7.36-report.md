# A7.36 — Playing Pathway confound diagnostic

**DIAGNOSTIC ONLY. No scoring, formula, weight, gate, UI or database change.** Production scoring
remains `0ae0399`. Raw measurements: `A7.36-measurements.json`.

**Verdict up front:** A7.26's confound is confirmed and it is **not the largest defect in the
component**. The largest is a **horizon collapse** that A7.26 could not see because it measured at a
single entry year. The repair class is **C — evidence/confidence**, not a formula defect.

---

## 1. The exact current formula

Verified against `shared/matching/v2/layers/opportunityComponents.js`, not assumed from the A7.8.2
summary. **The summary in the brief is exact**, with one correction worth stating:

```
pressure    = (starters + 0.4·squad + 0.5·unknown) / max(1, typicalStarters)
competition = 1 / (1 + pressure / 0.75)
rotation    = clamp01( (programmeShare − scale.p10) / (scale.p90 − scale.p10) )
pathway     = 0.6·competition + 0.4·rotation      ← only when BOTH are scoreable
            = whichever one is scoreable          ← otherwise
```

The fallback branch is not in the brief's summary and matters: **a pathway can be built from rotation
alone**, and 124 cells are (see §6, Defect 4).

**Playing Pathway is athlete-independent.** Its inputs are `sport`, `position`, `division`,
`programme`, `rosterOnFile` and `entryYear` — nothing else. Two athletes at the same position
entering the same year get identical values at every programme in the universe. A component named
for the *athlete's* opportunity varies with nothing about the athlete but their position and arrival
date. So the honest unit of analysis is the **programme-position cell**, and that is what §3 reports.

## 2. Input provenance

| input | where it comes from | what it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| `starters` | `bucket.starterLastSeason`, via `starterState(row)` on minutes/appearances | returners we could place as having **started this season** |
| `squad` | `total − starters − unknown`, residual | returners placed as **non-starters** — a derived class, never observed directly |
| `unknown` | `bucket.byLastSeasonUnknown` | returners whose **role** was unreadable. **Not** unreadable eligibility |
| `rotation` | `playingNorms.data.json` → `playingShareFor`, p10/p90 scaled | **effective players sharing the minutes**, from seasons already finished. A programme trait, identical for every athlete, blind to who will be there |
| `typicalStarters` | `recruitingRules.js` ← measured squad norms | GK 1 · DEF 4 · MID 5 · FWD 3 (men's) |
| `rosterOnFile` (rotation) | `rosterProgrammes.has(name)` | any roster row for the programme |
| `rosterOnFile` (competition) | `Boolean(bucket && bucket.rows > 0)` | **at least one row at this position** |

**The critical provenance fact.** `returningDepthFor` counts a player only if their eligibility
ceiling is **strictly after** the entry year. A row whose class label could not be read yields **no
ceiling at all** and enters neither the returning nor the departing count — it simply vanishes.
`bucket.unreadable` records how many did. **`returningCompetition` never receives it.**

## 4. Thin-roster confound — confirmed, with the mechanism named

High pathway means **"we counted few returners"**, and that has three quite different causes which
the component cannot tell apart:

1. **the group is genuinely small and young** — a real opportunity, and must stay rewarded;
2. **the group's rows were unreadable** — §5;
3. **the entry year is far enough out that nobody counts** — §5b, and the biggest of the three.

## 5. Data completeness — the less complete record wins, unanimously

**Matched comparison**, same division × position × rotation quintile, cells with ≥3 observed players,
fully-readable versus ≥50%-unreadable:

| | matched strata | median Δ pathway (less complete − complete) | strata where the less complete record scored higher |
|---|---|---|---|
| men's | 26 | **+0.233** | **26 / 26** |
| women's | 28 | **+0.234** | **28 / 28** |

**Unanimous in direction, and worth roughly the interquartile range of the whole distribution.**

The mechanism, exactly: a row with no readable class yields no eligibility ceiling, so it enters
neither `byLastSeason` nor `starterLastSeason`. `total` is therefore 0, and
`returningCompetition` then takes `coverage = 1` and `grade = MEASURED` because `total === 0`.
**An entirely unreadable position group scores the maximum and is graded as a measurement.**

Worked cases, all real:

| programme | position | rows observed | unreadable | pathway |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saint Joseph's (ME) | FORWARD | 10 | **10** | **1.000** |
| Wisconsin-La Crosse | DEFENSE | 12 | **12** | 0.953 |
| Northwestern | DEFENSE | 6 | **6** | 0.970 |
| Hamilton College | FORWARD | 6 | **6** | 0.940 |

64 men's and 74 women's cells score a perfect competition value with **nobody counted returning and
nobody counted departing** — only possible when no row in the group produced a ceiling.

> **`UNKNOWN` does become positive evidence, and it is graded MEASURED while doing it.** The one
> doubt the component *does* model — unknown **role** — it models correctly and in the safe
> direction (ρ −0.50 / −0.41; unknown returners *lower* the score). The doubt it does not model at
> all is unreadable **eligibility**, and that is the one that inverts.

**Scale, stated honestly: this affects ~1% of cells.** 349/28,083 men's and 296/32,388 women's
position rows are unreadable. The defect is severe and unanimous where it bites, and it is not by
itself a mass distortion.

### 5b. The horizon collapse — the largest defect, and A7.26 could not have seen it

Because a returner must have a ceiling **strictly after** the entry year, and the roster on file is
the 2026 season, the returning count decays as the entry year moves out. Share of scoreable men's
cells at `competition == 1.000`:

| entry year | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 | **2030** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| men's | 2.6% | 10.9% | 60.8% | **100.0%** |
| women's | 3.9% | 12.0% | 57.3% | **100.0%** |

**At a 2030 entry every cell in the universe scores exactly 1.000, and every one is graded
MEASURED.** The term carries 0.6 of Playing Pathway, orders nothing, and reports itself as a
measurement. At 2029 it is already 60%.

The component cannot grade its own decay because **it never learns the entry year** — the horizon is
applied by the caller before `returningCompetition` sees anything.

> **Latent, not currently firing.** Every athlete on file is a 2027 entrant (2.6%). The V3/W3
> validation profiles run at 2028 (10.9%). A Year-10 athlete would be a 2030 entrant. This is a
> defect waiting for an ordinary user, not a live fault — and it is why it survived A7.8.2, A7.25
> and A7.26, all of which measured at one horizon.

The existing code comment anticipated the *timing* limit — *"for an entry two years out the median
share of returners we can place is ZERO"* — and treated it as a **grade** problem. It is a **value**
problem: at distance the value itself becomes a constant.

## 6. Turnover — re-examined, and the rejection holds for a sharper reason

Re-ran `v2Turnover.js`. A7.10.2's confound reproduces exactly:

| | men's | women's |
|---|---|---|
| corr(departure **count**, position-group size) | **0.776** | **0.700** |
| corr(departure **share**, position-group size) | 0.032 | 0.004 |

**What exactly was confounded: the COUNT, and the share is clean of it.** That looks like an opening
— until repeatability:

| | men's | women's |
|---|---|---|
| split-half (early vs late), **count** | 0.608 | 0.504 |
| split-half, **share** | **0.123** | **−0.034** |
| adjacent-window, share | 0.028 · 0.010 · 0.137 | −0.047 · −0.036 · 0.022 |

**The repeatable part of turnover is the group-size part, and the part that is not group size is not
repeatable.** That is a stronger rejection than A7.10.2's: no narrower quantity rescues it, because
the two problems are complements rather than independent.

Further limits found:

- **Position-specific departures are reliable enough to compute and not to trust** — share stability
  is ≈0 at every position.
- **Class/year completeness is adequate** (1.2% / 0.9% of rows unreadable) — class labels are not the
  binding constraint. The binding constraint is that turnover share is nearly noise year to year.
- **Coverage is structurally missing for NAIA and USCAA**: 0 of 723 men's NAIA cells and 0 of 779
  women's have three or more transitions. **A turnover-based repair would be blind for ~15% of the
  men's universe**, which is a fairness problem, not just a coverage one.

**Graduating starters can be distinguished from generic departures** — `starterLastSeason` already
does it, and `positionalOpportunity` already consumes it as `vacatedStarters`. That is Recruitability's
question, and §7 says it should stay there.

## 7. Positional relationship — no double counting

Spearman between Playing Pathway and the **positional signal** (not the prior-mapped contribution):

| profile | ρ | ρ vs `vacatedStarters` |
|---|---|---|
| V3-B / V3-D | +0.101 | +0.140 |
| V3-C | +0.029 | +0.077 |
| W3-B / W3-D | +0.205 | +0.169 |
| W3-C | +0.095 | +0.134 |

**No double counting.** The correlations are weak and positive. They measure different sides of the
same index — who is projected to **stay** (Opportunity) against which starting places **empty**
(Recruitability) — and they share inputs without sharing an answer. The pre-Candidate-G failure mode
is **not** recreated.

**The disagreements are the interesting part**, and they point at §5 rather than at overlap:

| programme | pathway | positional | rows | unreadable | returning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Northwestern | 0.753 | **NONE_DETECTED** | 13 | **13** | 0 |
| Trevecca Nazarene | 0.894 | NONE_DETECTED | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| University of Mobile | 0.752 | UNKNOWN | 8 | 0 | 0 |

Northwestern is the clean one: **the same unreadable cohort makes Recruitability say "no opening
detected" and Opportunity say 0.753.**

> **The root cause is an asymmetry, and it is documented in the codebase already.**
> `positionalOpportunity` carries an explicit A7.7.2 guard — *"A ZERO MUST REST ON SOMETHING"* —
> added after it measured 307 men's and 341 women's cells scoring a confident zero on a departing
> cohort nobody could classify. It applies to the **departing** side. `returningCompetition` reads
> the **returning** side of the very same index and has **no equivalent guard**. The lesson was
> learned once and applied to one of the two sides.

## 8. Programme strength — mostly legitimate, and the probes prove it

corr(pathway, strength) is −0.225 men / −0.472 women. **It survives the completeness control
essentially untouched:**

| | all scoreable | fully-readable cells only |
|---|---|---|
| men's | −0.225 | **−0.223** |
| women's | −0.472 | **−0.456** |

So the strength lean is **not** a data-completeness artefact. It decomposes into two effects that are
both real:

- corr(returners, strength) = **+0.18 / +0.29** — stronger programmes genuinely retain more players;
- corr(rotation, strength) = −0.133 / −0.375 — weaker programmes genuinely share minutes more widely.

**A7.26's 45.7-versus-70.4 strength gap is reproduced** (top decile 0.373 vs first decile 0.714 in
percentile terms) **and reattributed**: it is mostly a real property of weaker programmes, not the
measurement artefact the phrasing implied.

**And the probes settle it.** Removing the competition term, removing rotation, doubling the unknown
weight and doubling the half-pressure constant **all leave top-100 median programme strength within
±1.8 points**, on every one of the six profiles. **Playing Pathway is not what makes V2 lean toward
weak programmes.** `pursuitRun.js` already says where that comes from — recruitability ceiling,
financial and opportunity all leaning the same way — and A7.36 confirms pathway is not the lever.

> This also **qualifies A7.25's concern**: rewarding pathway harder did worsen recommendations, but
> not by dragging strength down. It did it by promoting cells whose evidence was absent.

## 10. Shadow counterfactuals — instruments, not candidates

Six probes through the documented `opportunityOverrides` seam. **None is a proposed repair**, and
A7.36 selects no winner.

| probe | top-100 churn (range over 6 profiles) | Δ top-100 median strength | universe median pathway |
|---|---|---|---|
| `noCompetition` (rotation only) | 2 – 21 | −0.6 … +1.8 | −0.121 … −0.003 |
| `noRotation` (competition only) | 1 – 11 | −0.6 … +0.0 | +0.017 … +0.052 |
| `unknownWeight 1.0` | 0 – 9 | −0.4 … +0.1 | −0.069 … −0.080 |
| `halfAt 1.5` | 0 – 5 | +0.0 … +0.9 | +0.077 … +0.090 |
| `pathwayNeutral` | (the measuring stick, §9) | — | — |

What the probes locate:

- **The constants are not the problem.** `unknownWeight` and `halfAt` move the *level* of the
  distribution and barely move the *ordering* (churn ≤ 9). The confound survives every setting, so it
  is structural.
- **Neither half is disposable.** `noCompetition` churns most on the playing-priority profiles
  (19 and 21) — competition is doing real ordering work there, it is just doing it on evidence that
  is sometimes absent. `noRotation` churns less but removes the only term that works at a far horizon.
- **Strength is untouched by all of them** (§8).
- **Financial and Recruitability interaction: none observed.** Gate firing rates and the financial
  layer are unchanged under every probe, because pathway enters only Opportunity.

## 11. Root cause — **class C, evidence/confidence**, with a specific D component

| class | verdict |
|---|---|
| A — formula defect | **No.** Monotonicity, boundedness and the hyperbolic shape are all sound; the constants sweep changes nothing structural |
| B — data-quality defect | **Contributing, small.** ~1% of rows unreadable. Real, unanimous where it bites, not the main event |
| **C — evidence/confidence defect** | **PRIMARY.** The component cannot say *how much of the position group it actually read*, and treats "counted zero" as a measurement regardless of why |
| D — semantic-definition defect | **Secondary but genuine.** "Returning competition" is defined as a count with no horizon, so at distance it silently changes meaning from "how crowded" to "how many current first-years" |
| E — combination | in the sense that C and D share one root |

**The single sentence:** *a count of zero is being reported as a measurement of emptiness, when it may
equally be a measurement of our own blindness — and as the entry year moves out, blindness becomes
the normal case.*

## 12. Smallest repair families worth testing — **not implemented, not selected**

Listed in increasing size. A7.36 recommends **testing F1 and F2 first** because they are the smallest
things that could address class C, and deliberately does **not** rank them by expected effect.

- **F1 — pass the group's readability in, and let it reach coverage and grade.** `bucket.unreadable`
  and `bucket.rows` already exist at the call site and are already consumed by the other layer. The
  smallest possible change: no new data, no new formula, one more argument.
- **F2 — make the horizon visible.** Give `returningCompetition` the entry year and the roster season
  so it can degrade its own grade as the count decays. Answers §5b directly.
- **F3 — apply the A7.7.2 "a zero must rest on something" rule to the returning side**, mirroring the
  guard `positionalOpportunity` already carries. Structurally the most consistent with the codebase.
- **F4 — reconsider what the denominator should be at distance**, e.g. reading the position group's
  size rather than only its projected survivors. Largest, most semantic, most likely to need its own
  preregistration.

**F0 — fix the rotation-only coverage report** (0.6 where 0.4 is correct, §6 Defect 4). Independent of
all of the above and small enough to travel with any of them.

## 13. What should NOT be changed

- **Do not stop rewarding thin position groups.** A group we have read completely and found to carry
  one returner genuinely *is* a better opportunity. A test asserts this so a repair cannot overcorrect.
- **Do not penalise weak programmes.** §8 shows their advantage is mostly real, and the probes show
  pathway is not what produces V2's strength lean.
- **Do not move positional need into Opportunity.** §7 finds no double counting; creating some would
  reproduce the pre-Candidate-G failure.
- **Do not adopt turnover.** §6 closes it more firmly than A7.10.2 did, and it is unavailable for
  NAIA and USCAA entirely.
- **Do not introduce a hard exclusion** on inferred roster availability. Asserted in the test file.
- **Do not retune the constants.** §10 shows they are not carrying the defect.
- **Do not touch Candidate G, the A7.10/A7.11 positional line, majorFit 1.20, or the A7.29 boundary.**

## 17. Recommended A7.37 scope

**A7.37 should repair class C only, and should preregister before measuring.**

1. **Preregister** acceptance conditions for F1 and F2 — in particular what must happen to the 26/26
   and 28/28 matched strata, and to the 100%-at-maximum figure at a 2030 entry.
2. **Implement F1** — pass `positionRows` and `unreadable` into `returningCompetition`; let them
   reach `coverage` and `grade`. Do not change the value formula.
3. **Implement F2** — pass the entry year and roster season; degrade the grade as the count decays.
4. **Re-measure** the §5 matched comparison, the §5b horizon table and the §9 classification.
5. **Leave the value formula, the constants, the weights and the gates alone**, and leave §13 intact.

**A7.37 should NOT** attempt F4, adopt turnover, or touch the strength lean — that lean is real and,
if it is to be addressed at all, belongs to a level-preference phase rather than to Playing Pathway.
