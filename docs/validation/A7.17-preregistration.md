# A7.17 — preregistration

**Written and committed before any reach candidate was measured.** Baselines quoted are Candidate
G's, already measured and committed at `cf04ee1`. G is the provisional repaired baseline: every
candidate below runs **on top of G**, and G itself is the control.

Nothing here changes production. Sign convention throughout is the codebase's:
`delta = athletePercentile − programmePercentile`, so **a reach is `delta < 0`**.

---

## 1. The mechanism

```
delta <  0   A = a0 · exp(delta / decay)        a0 = 0.80   decay = 0.08
delta >= 0   A = a0 + (1 − a0)·(1 − exp(−delta / rise))     rise  = 0.08
```

`R = A × (φ + (1−φ)·support)` and A multiplies, so A collapsing collapses R and therefore Pursuit.
Measured: Virginia sits ~9.7 percentile points above the V3-B athlete and carries **A = 0.238** —
a 70% collapse of the ceiling over ten percentile points. No candidate in A7.16, G included, put a
single above-level programme in any top 100.

## 2. What uncertainty information actually exists — measured, not invented

The calibration file `abilityScale.data.json` (`ability-scale-2026-09-19`, 1069 programmes,
nearest-rank) carries **no** standard error, confidence interval, variance or distributional
field. Its keys are `programmes, min, max, sourceDistributionDigest, divisionCounts, distribution,
ratings`, and each rating carries only `rating, percentile, soccerScore, divisionMix`. The
rating→percentile map is **declared**, in the module's own words, not estimated.

What *is* measurable is the **quantisation of the athlete estimate**:

| rating | percentile | step from previous |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 78.0 | +14.0 |
| 8 | 89.0 | +11.0 |
| **9** | **96.0** | **+7.0** |
| 10 | 99.5 | +3.5 |

The input is an integer 1–10 operator judgement. For a rating-9 athlete the nearest distinguishable
alternative estimates are 7 percentile points below and 3.5 above. **`COMPATIBILITY_DECAY = 0.08`
is 8 percentile points — approximately one rating step.**

So the architecture currently treats a programme *less than one unit of its own input resolution*
above the athlete as carrying a factor-of-e penalty. That is not a claim that reaches should be
free; it is an observation that the curve resolves distinctions its input cannot support. This
phase does **not** invent a confidence value. Candidate R-D uses only the measured step width.

**Requires future data**, recorded here and not used: any genuine uncertainty treatment needs
either repeated independent ratings of the same athlete, or outcome data linking rating to realised
destination. Neither exists.

## 3. Reach bands — defined before results

In percentile space, from the measured quantisation above:

| band | definition | rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `AT_OR_BELOW` | `delta ≥ 0` | athlete at or above the programme |
| `REALISTIC_REACH` | `−0.07 ≤ delta < 0` | inside one rating step at the elite end |
| `AMBITIOUS_REACH` | `−0.15 ≤ delta < −0.07` | one to roughly two rating steps |
| `EXTREME_REACH` | `delta < −0.15` | beyond two rating steps |

## 4. Candidates — all applied on top of G, reach side (`delta < 0`) only

| id | formula for `delta < 0` | mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **G** (control) | `0.80·exp(delta/0.08)` | A7.16 winner, unchanged |
| **R-A** | `0.80·exp(delta/0.16)` | slower decay: half-life doubled |
| **R-B** | `ρ + (0.80−ρ)·exp(delta/0.08)`, ρ = 0.20 | bounded floor; strictly monotone, asymptote not a flat cutoff |
| **R-C** | piecewise decay 0.20 / 0.10 / 0.05 across the three reach bands, constants chosen for continuity | realistic / ambitious / extreme treated as different regimes |
| **R-D** | mean of current A over `delta ∈ [delta−h, delta+h]`, `h` = half the local rating step (measured) | uncertainty-aware: the estimate is quantised, so integrate over its resolution |
| **R-E** | `0.80·exp(delta/(0.08·(1+κ·p)))`, κ = 0.8, `p` = positional value (0 when zero/unknown) | positive roster evidence partially compensates a moderate reach; exponential still kills an extreme one |
| **R-F** | `0.80 / (1 + (−delta/h)^n)`, h = 0.12, n = 2 | heavier-tailed monotone alternative; "half weight at 12 percentile points" is explainable |
| **CTRL-BAD-R1** | `A = 0.80` for all `delta < 0` | no reach penalty — must be rejected |
| **CTRL-BAD-R2** | G, plus `P *= (1 + 0.5·strengthPercentile)` | prestige boost — must be rejected |
| **CTRL-BAD-R3** | G, plus drop every `delta < −0.15` | hard reach cutoff — must be rejected |

The `delta ≥ 0` side is **not** touched by any candidate. Overqualification is still never penalised.

## 5. Acceptance conditions — frozen

Baselines in brackets are Candidate G's, from `cf04ee1`.

### Reach behaviour
- **RA1** realistic reaches enter the slice: **≥ 5** in the top 100 in **at least two** of V3-A /
  V3-B / V3-F
- **RA2** above-level programmes no longer structurally excluded: **≥ 1** reach of any band in the
  top 100 in **all three** profiles [G: 0 in all three]
- **RA3** extreme reaches stay meaningfully penalised: median rank of `EXTREME_REACH` worse than
  median rank of `REALISTIC_REACH` by **≥ 150 places in every profile**, and extreme reaches
  **≤ 5%** of any top 100
- **RA4** at-level keeps its advantage: median rank of `delta ∈ [0, 0.07)` better than median rank
  of `REALISTIC_REACH` in every profile, **and** `A(0) > A(δ)` for all `δ < 0` checked on the
  formula

### G must not regress
- **RB1** V3-B substantially below **≤ 65%** and near-or-above **≥ 5%** [G: 11%, 16%]
- **RB2** V3-A substantially below **≤ 55%** [G: 0%]
- **RB3** Financial intact: `corr(P,F) ≥ 0.60` on V3-F **and ≥ 20** top-100 programmes with
  Δstrength < −15 and F ≥ 0.60 [G: +0.739, 27]
- **RB4** preference response intact: level-focused vs playing-first gap **≥ 10pp** [G: 77pp]
- **RB5** positional ordering intact: `zero < unknown < positive` medians with unknown and zero
  **≥ 50** places apart in every profile, and **≥ 5** measured-zero programmes in the top 100 in
  **≥ 2** profiles [G: passes]
- **RB6** developmental control: V3-D near-or-above within **5pp** of G [G: 71%]

### Structural
- **RC1** `A(delta)` monotone non-decreasing and continuous on the whole real line — checked on the
  formula, numerically on a fine grid
- **RC2** no hard cutoff: every reach band with ≥ 10 members in the universe must have at least one
  programme inside the **top 500** in every profile
- **RD1** V1 `snapshot:matching --check` and `snapshot:pi --check` report 0 differences

## 6. Declared in advance

- Named programmes (Virginia, UCLA, Stanford) are **diagnostic only**. No parameter is tuned to
  place them. Every condition above is distributional.
- Evaluator containment, Kendall tau and pairwise agreement are **reported and used in no
  condition**, as at A7.16.
- Parameters (`decay_reach`, ρ, the piecewise constants, κ, h, n) are set once, here, from the
  reasoning given. They will not be swept to find a pass. A candidate failing only on a parameter
  value is reported as a failure with that observation.
- If no candidate passes, the phase says so and no winner is forced.
- If several pass, the **smallest and most explainable** is recommended, not the best-scoring.
