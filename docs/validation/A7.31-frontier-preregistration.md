**13 usable labels.** Both exclusions are frozen here and are not revisited after results.

**U-14 is excluded on category, not on inconvenience.** All three independents chose PRE_READ there
for a **governing-body eligibility** reason (a 1.0 GPA against the NCAA D1 core-GPA floor), not an
institutional-admissions reason. Fitting an admissions frontier to it would force the
acceptance-rate rule — which correctly clears open-admission institutions — to absorb a signal that
belongs to a different dimension entirely. That is the category error A7.31 exists to prevent, and
a boundary distorted to cover it would be wrong for every other high-acceptance programme.

**U-15 is excluded because there is no majority to fit.** Its three-way split is a vocabulary
problem, not evidence about where a frontier sits.

### The honest weaknesses of this label set, stated before use

1. **n = 13.** This can discriminate among a *small number of interpretable families*. It cannot fit
   a curve and nothing here will pretend otherwise.
2. **Badly imbalanced: 10 PRE_READ, 2 VERIFY_EARLY, 1 NORMAL.** The VERIFY band is defined by two
   points. Any family's VERIFY/PRE_READ frontier above ~29% acceptance is essentially unconstrained
   by this data.
3. **No labels between 40% and 71% acceptance**, and only one above 45%.
4. **The pack was built by the boundary's author**, even though the labels were not.

## 2. Candidate families — frozen

Only interpretable forms. No high-dimensional model.

| id | family | parameters and ranges |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **constant cut — the control.** PRE_READ iff `shortfall ≤ −K` and `acceptance < C` | `K ∈ {250, 275, 300, 325, 350}`, `C` fixed at the current 0.50 |
| **B** | **stepped acceptance bands.** A shortfall cut per band, bands fixed at `<10%`, `10–20%`, `20–35%`, `35–50%` | each band's cut drawn from `{225, 250, 275, 300, 325, 350, 375, 400}`, **constrained to be non-decreasing across bands** |
| **C** | **simple monotone frontier.** PRE_READ iff `shortfall ≤ −(a + b·acceptance)` | `a ∈ {200, 225, 250, 275}`, `b ∈ {0, 100, 200, 300, 400}` — `b = 0` reproduces family A and is retained as an internal control |

The `acceptance ≥ 0.50` clearance and the `shortfall ≥ −50` clearance are **not swept**. They are
the source of the zero-over-escalation record across A7.28–A7.30 and are held fixed.

The VERIFY/NORMAL boundary is **not swept**. Only the VERIFY→PRE_READ frontier is under study.

## 3. Acceptance criteria — frozen

A candidate is **admissible** only if it satisfies all of:

- **S1 — ZERO OVER-ESCALATION.** No card whose independent majority is NORMAL or VERIFY_EARLY may be
  assigned a stricter level. **This is a hard constraint, not a term in an objective.** A candidate
  violating it is rejected however much under-escalation it removes.
- **S2 — NORMAL preserved.** Every independent NORMAL must remain NORMAL.
- **S3 — monotone.** The required shortfall must not decrease as acceptance rises (families B and C
  by construction; checked for A).
- **S4 — bounded change.** Total concern set (VERIFY ∪ PRE_READ) across the 56 A7.28 ladder runs must
  stay within **±15%** of today's 563. A frontier is a re-levelling instrument; a candidate that
  materially grows or shrinks the flagged population is doing something else.

Among admissible candidates, rank by:

- **Primary — under-escalation count**: the number of independent-PRE_READ labels the candidate
  assigns to VERIFY_EARLY. Lower is better. Today's boundary scores 6 on this set.
- **Tie-break 1 — fewest parameters**, then **fewest distinct cut values**. A tie goes to the
  simpler and more explainable form.
- **Tie-break 2 — smallest change to the A7.28 ladder population.**

**The objective is not maximum recall.** S1 is inviolable, and a candidate that reaches zero
under-escalation by escalating a single VERIFY label is rejected outright.

## 4. Insufficient evidence — frozen

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and `PARTIAL`/`UNSCOREABLE` cards are **outside the study**. No candidate may
change how they are handled: PRE_READ remains unreachable without MEASURED evidence, and partial
evidence may still clear a case and never condemn one. No independent evaluator returned
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE on any of the 15 cards, so there is nothing to fit here and nothing will be
changed here.

## 5. Calibration is not validation — the reuse warning

> **If the A7.30 labels are used to SELECT among these families, they become CALIBRATION evidence for
> the selected frontier and cease to be VALIDATION evidence for it.**

A frontier chosen against these 13 labels is **fitted**, not validated, however good its score. Its
apparent under-escalation count on this set is an in-sample number and must never be reported as a
validation result.

**Therefore: any frontier selected by this study requires a NEW independent holdout — a fresh pack,
fresh independent evaluators, labels not used in selection — before it may be described as
validated, and before it may be shown to anyone outside the operator surface.**

This is stated here, before the study runs, so the conclusion cannot be quietly upgraded afterwards.

## 6. Declared in advance

- No card is added, removed or relabelled after results are seen. The two exclusions above are final.
- If no candidate satisfies S1–S4, **the current boundary stands** and the study reports that.
- If several are admissible and tie on under-escalation, the simplest wins — not the most responsive.
- The study changes no code. It reports which family the independent evidence prefers.
