# A7.30 — pack selection protocol

**Written and committed BEFORE the pack was generated.** Nothing in this phase changes production,
the shadow boundary, or any data.

## The rule

15 cards. For each cell below, take the **best-ranked qualifying programme** across all 56 A7.28
ladder runs, ties broken by programme name ascending, **no institution used twice**, and no
institution used in the A7.27, A7.28 or A7.29 packs.

Cells are defined on **acceptance rate and SAT shortfall only** — quantities that exist in the
database independently of any assessment. No cell references a shadow outcome.

| # | cell |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | contested: acceptance 5–10% × shortfall ≈ −250, −300, −350, −400 |
| 5–7 | contested: acceptance 10–20% × shortfall ≈ −250, −300, −400 |
| 8–10 | contested: acceptance 20–30% × shortfall ≈ −250, −300, −400 |
| 11–12 | contested: acceptance 30–45% × shortfall ≈ −300, −400 |
| 13 | control: shortfall ≥ 0, any acceptance |
| 14 | control: shortfall ≤ −500 at acceptance ≥ 80% |
| 15 | control: |shortfall| ≤ 60 at acceptance < 12% |

Where no real record exists for a cell, **the cell is left empty rather than manufactured** and the
shortfall is taken from the nearest band with a real record, recorded as such.

**Correction, made before any evaluator saw the pack and recorded rather than quietly applied.** The
sentence above was under-specified in its first draft: "nearest real record" was implemented as
*best-ranked within a widened shortfall band*, which on the first generation returned a −284 record
for a −400 cell and cost the pack the severe-mismatch coverage §2 of the brief requires. The rule is
now **nearest in shortfall to the cell's target midpoint**, ties broken by rank then name. Exclusion
of previously-used institutions is also keyed on **`unitid`** rather than on the programme name,
because the two sports spell some institutions differently ("MIT" against "Massachusetts Institute
of Technology") and a name key let one through. Neither correction references a shadow outcome.

**Sport balance** is enforced as a tie-break, not as a filter: where a cell has both men's and
women's candidates, alternate by cell index so the pack carries both. Major match and non-match,
athletic level and financial situation are **whatever the selected records carry** — they are
reported, never selected on.

## Declared in advance

- No card is chosen because it flatters or embarrasses the current boundary.
- **Honest limitation, stated before generating:** the author knows the current shadow rule, so a
  grid defined on acceptance and shortfall implicitly knows what the shadow would output. The
  mitigation is uniform grid coverage rather than cell choice, and the selection is mechanical.
- The pack is frozen by digest before any evaluator sees it and its factual contents do not change
  afterwards.
- The current A7.29 shadow logic is **frozen exactly**: no SAT cut, no stepped band, no frontier
  change, no GPA scoring, no `test_policy` logic, no evidence-state change.
