# A7.16 — preregistration

**Written and committed before any candidate was measured.** The only numbers below that come
from measurement are the production baselines already reported in A7.12–A7.15 and committed at
`2ded421`, `7da4f19`, `3b750ae` and `a454f24`.

Nothing in this phase changes production. Every candidate runs in a sandbox that re-scores the
universe from the same frozen pool (`86b911af2515ab10`) and never writes a scorer file.

---

## 1. The defect, restated

```
R = A × (φ + (1 − φ) × support)        φ = 0.35
support = Σ over KNOWN signals of w_s · v_s      w = {positional 0.75, market 0.25}
```

An unknown signal contributes nothing and its weight is not redistributed (A7.7.4). So when
positional is unknown **or measured zero**, `support ≤ 0.25 · market ≤ 0.25`, hence
`R ≤ A × 0.5125`, and the observed maximum across 858 programmes is **0.497**.

Measured across three profiles:

| | V3-A | V3-B | V3-F |
|---|---|---|---|
| best rank, measured-zero positional | #167 | #328 | #129 |
| best rank, unscoreable positional | #199 | #388 | #118 |
| **either, inside top 100** | **0** | **0** | **0** |
| programmes affected | 220 / 858 (26%) | same | same |

This violates operator principles 4 and 5: a measured zero structurally excludes, and unknown
behaves indistinguishably from a measured zero (best ranks #167 vs #199, #328 vs #388, #129 vs
#118 — the same place).

**Repair objective:** prevent uncertain or inferred roster need from overpowering overall
athlete–programme suitability, while keeping it meaningful evidence. *Not* "make programme
strength determine ranking."

---

## 2. Locked recruitment theory (operator-confirmed)

1. Level is a strong anchor, **not** a hard constraint or band.
2. Athlete context (budget, academics, preferences) may legitimately override level.
3. Positive positional need is valuable evidence and must keep real ranking effect.
4. A measured zero is at most **mild negative evidence**; it must not structurally exclude.
5. Unknown positional need is **uncertainty, not negative evidence**, and must stay semantically
   distinct from a measured zero.
6. Explicit coach intelligence would be far stronger evidence. **Not implemented here**; the
   architecture must merely leave room for it.

Derived ordering requirement, which every candidate is judged against:

```
measured zero  <  unknown  <  any positive positional evidence
```

A measured zero may cost something. Unknown must cost nothing and gain nothing — it sits at the
neutral prior. This is **not** the A7.7.3 renormalisation failure: under renormalisation an
unknown signal's weight is absorbed by the surviving signal, so unknown could outrank
*measured-positive*. Here unknown takes a neutral prior **in its own slot** and can never exceed
a positive measurement.

## 3. Candidate families

Each is defined before measurement. `p` = positional value, `m` = market value, `π` = neutral
prior for an unmeasured signal, `ζ` = floor, `λ` = zero-evidence penalty.

| id | family | formula change | mechanism | defect addressed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CTRL-0** | do-nothing | production, unchanged | baseline | — |
| **CTRL-BAD-1** | hard band | drop every programme with Δ < −15 | must be rejected | — |
| **CTRL-BAD-2** | no positional | `support = m` (market only) | must be rejected | — |
| **CTRL-BAD-3** | strength rules | `P = 0.7·strengthPercentile + 0.3·P_prod` | must be rejected | — |
| **A** | known-signal renormalisation | `support = Σw·v / Σw` over known | removes the unknown ceiling | ceiling (unknown only) |
| **B** | support floor | positional contributes `w_p·(ζ + (1−ζ)·p)`, ζ = 0.30 | a measured zero cannot collapse support | ceiling (zero + unknown) |
| **C** | positive-evidence asymmetry | unknown → `w_p·π`; zero → `w_p·π·(1−λ)`; `p>0` → `w_p·(π + (1−π)·p)`; π = 0.45, λ = 0.35 | encodes principles 3–5 directly | ceiling, zero≠unknown |
| **D1** | additive decoupling | `R = 0.60·A + 0.40·S_C` (S_C = C's support) | A stops being a multiplicative ceiling | ceiling + saturation |
| **D2** | floor lift only | φ = 0.35 → 0.55, else unchanged | minimal structural change | ceiling |
| **E** | level alignment term | `align = exp(−(δ/σ)²)`, σ = 0.18 in percentile space | separates *can play here* from *aligned with level*; smooth, never zero | A saturation |
| **F** | preference-scaled level authority | `P *= 1 − τ(pri)·(1 − align)`, τ = {1:0.10, 2:0.20, 3:0.30, 4:0.40, 5:0.50} | level authority outside R, scaled by the athlete's own statement | level authority + preference sensitivity |
| **G** | combined minimal | C + F | smallest combination expected to clear the bar | all confirmed defects |
| **G-lite** | C alone | — | tests whether F is necessary | — |
| **G-alt** | B + F | — | tests whether C's asymmetry beats a plain floor | — |

`align` uses the same percentile-space delta as athletic plausibility, so it introduces no new
data dependency. σ = 0.18 is chosen so a programme ~15 strength points from the athlete retains
roughly half weight — a soft anchor, never a band. τ never reaches 1, so F can never zero a
programme: principle 1 holds by construction.

## 4. Acceptance conditions — frozen

A candidate **passes** only if it satisfies every condition. Production baselines in brackets.

### A. Elite balanced (V3-B) — the primary target
- **A1** top-100 substantially below (Δ < −15) **≤ 65%** [92%]
- **A2** top-100 near level or above (Δ ≥ −3) **≥ 5%** [0%]
- **A3** top-25 substantially below **≤ 80%** [100%]

> **On the ≤60% figure I proposed at A7.15.** I do not freeze it, and the reason matters.
> Production V3-A — the athlete who states level at 5 of 5 — sits at 66%. Requiring the
> *neutral* athlete to reach <60% would demand that stating nothing outperforms stating the
> maximum, which inverts principle 1 and quietly converts "remove the ceiling" into "make level
> dominate". I freeze **65%** for V3-B and enforce the level preference separately through
> condition C, which requires V3-A to be materially better than V3-B. 65% is not a feasibility
> limit: 164 of 858 programmes are within 15 points of this athlete and V3-B has no financial
> constraint, so a much lower figure is reachable — it is a *sufficiency* threshold, deliberately
> loose, so that clearing it means the pathology is gone rather than that level has taken over.

### B. Financially constrained (V3-F) — Financial must not regress
- **B1** F-gate still fires on **≥ 80%** of the ranked pool [85%]
- **B2** `corr(P, F)` **≥ 0.60** [+0.778]
- **B3** R and O remain **bit-identical** between the V3-B and V3-F runs (contribution is the
  sole scoring input) [max\|Δ\| = 0]
- **B4** of the 6 programmes that are near level *and* affordable *and* inside production's top
  100, **at least 5 remain inside the top 100**
- **B5** affordable substantially-below programmes must still be able to rise: at least **20** of
  the top 100 have Δ < −15 **and** F ≥ 0.60 [28]

> B5 is a floor, not a ceiling. Per the brief, "higher strength is always better" is explicitly
> **not** the success criterion for V3-F. When the universe does not contain enough affordable
> near-level programmes — and it does not: near-level median F is 0.304 against 0.384 for the
> substantially-below population, with only 9 of 164 near-level programmes at F ≥ 0.60 — the
> right list goes down-level, and a candidate must not prevent that.

### C. Level-focused (V3-A) — preference sensitivity
- **C1** V3-A top-100 substantially below **≤ 55%** [66%]
- **C2** V3-A must be **at least 8 percentage points better than V3-B** on that measure
  [currently V3-A 66% vs V3-B 92%, so production already satisfies C2 — it is retained so that a
  candidate cannot pass by flattening the preference response]
- **C3** playing-opportunity sensitivity: a 5/1/3 and a 1/5/3 athlete must differ by **≥ 10
  percentage points** on top-100 substantially-below share

### D. Measured-zero positional
- **D1** measured zero no longer structurally excludes: **≥ 5** of the 161 measured-zero
  programmes inside the top 100 in **at least two** of the three profiles [0 in all three]
- **D2** positive positional evidence keeps meaningful effect: median rank of positive-positional
  programmes better than median rank of measured-zero by **≥ 100 places**, in **every** profile
  [currently 338 vs 726, 320 vs 700, 361 vs 527 — production passes D2 and must not lose it]

### E. Unknown positional
- **E1** unknown and measured zero must not behave identically: median rank difference **≥ 50
  places** in every profile [currently 686 vs 726, 731 vs 700, 566 vs 527 — production **fails**
  E1 on two of three]
- **E2** ordering must be `zero ≤ unknown`: median rank of unknown **better than or equal to**
  measured zero in every profile
- **E3** unknown must never outrank a *positive* measurement of the same signal, all else equal —
  checked analytically on the formula, not statistically

### F. Developmental control (V3-D)
- **F1** V3-D top-100 near-level-or-above share must not fall by more than **5 percentage
  points** from the do-nothing control
- **F2** V3-D top-100 substantially-below share must not rise by more than **10 percentage
  points**

> V3-D has no evaluator answer set and will not get one. It is a distribution-shape control only:
> the elite repair must not be bought by damaging matching for weaker athletes.

### G. No new cliff
- **G1** no candidate may introduce a discontinuity: for each candidate, the mapping from
  positional value to R must be monotone non-decreasing and continuous on (0, 1]
- **G2** no candidate may produce a new structural exclusion — no evidence state may have **zero**
  representation in the top 100 across all three profiles

### H. Compatibility
- **H1** V1 `snapshot:matching --check` and `snapshot:pi --check` report 0 differences
- **H2** the V2 test suite passes unchanged

## 5. What will NOT be optimised

Evaluator agreement (containment, Kendall tau, pairwise agreement) is **reported for every
candidate and used in no acceptance condition**. The frozen evaluator's YES/NO separates almost
perfectly on programme strength in all three packs, its correlation with playing-pathway evidence
is −0.089, and it moved its own threshold 15 strength points between V3-B and V3-F. It is
directional evidence about whether a list is defensible, not ground truth, and no parameter will
be searched to maximise it.

Selection is on: architectural coherence with the locked theory, monotonic behaviour, robustness
across all four profiles, removal of structural ceilings, and explainability.

---

## 6. Declared in advance

- If no candidate satisfies every condition, the phase reports that and **no winner is forced**.
- If several pass, the **smallest** is recommended, not the best-scoring.
- Parameters (π, ζ, λ, σ, τ, φ) are set once, here, from the stated reasoning. They will not be
  swept to find a pass. If a candidate fails only on a parameter value, that is reported as a
  failure with the observation, not repaired in place.
